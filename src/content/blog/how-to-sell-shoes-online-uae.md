Before working out how to sell shoes online, UAE brands need to accept one fact: their customers do not share a size system. A shopper from Manchester thinks in UK sizes, one from Milan in EU sizes and one from Chicago in US sizes, while plenty of residents switch between all three depending on the brand. Add a climate where sandals are worn most of the year, strong demand for modest and traditional styles, and buyers who expect cash on delivery and fast exchanges, and the usual footwear playbook needs reworking. This guide takes you from range planning to a launch checklist.

## Step 1: Match your range to how the UAE dresses

Start by mapping your styles to real buying occasions here, not the seasons of a cooler country.

- **Traditional Arabic sandals.** Worn with a kandura for work, prayer and family visits. Buyers care about leather, stitching, sole comfort over long days and a clean finish.
- **Modest and occasion footwear for women.** Flats, mules, loafers and block heels chosen to go with an abaya, plus dressier pairs for weddings and Eid.
- **Breathable trainers and gym shoes.** Air-conditioned malls and gyms mean trainers sell all year, but mesh uppers and light soles matter for the walk outside.
- **Beach, pool and casual sandals.** Quick-drying and easy to clean.
- **School shoes.** Many schools set strict rules on colour and style, and families buy in a rush before term starts in late August.

Choose one or two of these to lead with. A brand that is known for one thing gets cheaper clicks and more repeat buyers than one that sells a little of everything.

### Research before you order stock

Read reviews on Namshi, Amazon.ae and Noon for the styles you plan to sell. Comments about sizing running small, soles slipping on marble floors, straps rubbing in the heat or leather smelling strong in a closed box tell you what to fix and what to explain on the page. Instagram comments on rival brands' posts are another honest source.

## Step 2: Build size charts that work across EU, UK and US systems

Size confusion causes more exchanges than any other problem, and every exchange in the UAE means another courier trip.

### How to build the chart

1. Measure the inside length of each last, or have your factory supply it.
2. Ask shoppers to measure their foot from heel to longest toe, standing, in centimetres.
3. Map each foot length to your EU, UK and US size for that specific style family.
4. Add width guidance: narrow, standard or wide.
5. Add a fit note taken from your exchange data and reviews.

### What the chart should show

| Column | What to include |
|---|---|
| Foot length (cm) | The anchor, measured heel to toe |
| EU size | For shoppers from Europe, the Arab world and much of Asia |
| UK size | For British and many South Asian shoppers |
| US size | Separate men's and women's columns |
| Fit note | "Runs small", "true to size" or "roomy", per style |

Never reuse one generic conversion table for the whole range. Two lasts labelled EU 40 can differ noticeably in length.

### Children's and school shoes

Show kids' sizes by foot length with a rough age band, explain how much growing room each style allows, and say plainly whether a school shoe meets common uniform rules such as all-black uppers. Parents buying in a hurry need that answer in one glance.

## Step 3: Write product pages for the heat and two languages

Shoppers cannot touch the shoe, so the page has to do it for them.

- **Materials and climate.** Say how the upper and lining feel in heat, whether the shoe breathes and how the sole grips on smooth floors.
- **On-foot photos and a walking clip.** Show the shoe from the side, back and sole, and on a real foot with everyday clothes and with traditional dress where relevant.
- **Care advice.** Leather sandals and suede need different care in dust and humidity.
- **Arabic and English.** Build right-to-left layouts properly, and have the Arabic written or checked by a fluent Arabic writer, not just machine translated.

## Step 4: Choose where to sell

### Your own Shopify store

Your store holds the full range, the exchange process, customer data and your margin. Offer cash on delivery, cards, Apple Pay and Tabby or Tamara instalments on higher-priced pairs, and add a WhatsApp button for size questions.

### Namshi

Namshi suits fashion-led styles and trend browsing. Its listing standards are strict, so prepare consistent images, size mapping and descriptions before you apply.

### Amazon.ae and Noon

These reach shoppers who search by brand, style and price, and they matter for school shoes and everyday sandals. Map your sizes carefully to each platform's fields so a UK 8 never appears as an EU 8. Our guide on [how to sell on Noon](/blog/how-to-sell-on-noon/) covers seller registration.

### Retail context

Mall chains such as Centrepoint shape what shoppers expect on price and range. Most growing brands compete on design, comfort, niche styles and better online service rather than trying to match them.

## Step 5: Control cash on delivery, returns and exchanges

Shoes are especially exposed to refused parcels, because shoppers may doubt the size or change their mind at the door.

### COD controls

- Send a WhatsApp confirmation for every COD order with the size, colour and delivery day.
- Flag repeat refusals and ask those customers to prepay.
- Offer a small reason to pay online on second orders.
- Check what each refusal costs with the [RTO and COD calculator](/tools/rto-cod-calculator/).

### Exchange terms

Put the exchange policy on every product page, not only in the footer. State the time limit, condition rules, who pays for a size swap and how quickly the new pair ships. Make sure your terms match UAE consumer protection rules; we are not lawyers, so check them with an adviser. Track exchange reasons by style and size, and fix the chart or fit note when the same reason repeats.

## Step 6: Delivery and packaging

Dubai and Abu Dhabi shoppers are used to next-day or same-day delivery in many areas, so show a realistic delivery day on the product page and at checkout. Use a sturdy shoe box inside a mailer so the box arrives clean, since many buyers keep it for exchanges or gifting. Arrange courier pickup for exchanges with your delivery partner rather than asking customers to drop parcels off.

## Step 7: Run ads on a clean catalogue

### Feed checklist

- Each size listed as its own variant, with live stock.
- Size system stated in the feed, plus gender, age group, colour and material.
- Titles that include style, material and the words shoppers use, such as "men's leather sandals".
- Sold-out sizes removed from ads automatically.

### Where to advertise

Meta and Snapchat catalogue ads work well for retargeting browsers, TikTok suits on-foot styling videos, and Google Shopping catches people searching for a style by name. Run creative in Arabic and English and judge results on pairs kept after exchanges and refusals, not on orders placed.

## Step 8: Turn reviews and repeat buyers into a sizing advantage

Footwear reviews do two jobs. They persuade new shoppers, and they tell you how each style really fits. Ask for a review a week or so after delivery, once the shoes have been worn outside, and include one simple question: did the size feel small, true or large? Summarise those answers as a fit note on the product page and update it as more replies arrive.

Answer questions on your store, Namshi, Amazon.ae and Noon quickly and in the language they were asked in. A clear reply about width or arch support helps the next ten shoppers with the same doubt.

Repeat buyers are where footwear margin grows. A shopper who kept a pair already knows their size in your lasts, so remember it in their account and use it in follow-up messages: "your size 42 is back in tan". Parents who bought school shoes last August will need the next size up next year, so a reminder before term starts is genuinely useful. Use WhatsApp only for people who agreed to hear from you, and keep messages short.

### Business basics

You will need a trade licence that covers online retail, from the mainland or a free zone depending on how and where you sell, plus VAT registration at 5% once you pass the threshold. Ask your customs broker whether imported shoes need country-of-origin or Arabic labelling for your categories. We are not lawyers or accountants, so confirm these points with local advisers.

## Common mistakes

- One generic size chart for every style.
- Ads that keep showing styles whose common sizes have sold out.
- Hidden or unclear exchange terms.
- No order confirmation for COD purchases.
- Arabic pages built by machine translation alone.

## A simple launch checklist

1. Pick a lead category and order a focused first range.
2. Build foot-length charts per style with EU, UK and US columns.
3. Photograph and film every style on foot.
4. Launch your store in Arabic and English with COD, cards and instalments.
5. List on Namshi, Amazon.ae or Noon with careful size mapping.
6. Set up WhatsApp confirmations and an exchange process.
7. Start catalogue ads and review net sales after exchanges each week.

Once the UAE store runs smoothly, Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC share many sizing habits and marketplaces, which makes expansion more straightforward.
