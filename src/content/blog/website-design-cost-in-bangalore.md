The website design cost in Bangalore ranges from about ₹30,000 for a simple WordPress site to ₹2 lakh or more for a custom corporate website, and higher again for a custom online store. Where you land depends on what the site must do, how much is designed from scratch, and how much content you already have. This guide gives you real starting prices, explains what moves the number, and shows how to compare quotes from Bengaluru freelancers and agencies without overpaying.

## Website design cost in Bangalore by website type

The table below uses our own starting prices. Treat them as a benchmark when you compare quotes. Every project is scoped in writing before work starts, so the final figure reflects your actual pages and features.

| Website type | Starting price (one-time) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| WordPress website | From ₹30,000 | Startups, consultants, clinics and service firms that need a clean, editable site |
| Business / corporate website (5–10 pages) | From ₹35,000 | Small and mid-size companies that need a credible site with services, about, careers and contact pages |
| Custom corporate website | ₹75,000–2 lakh | Companies with a strong brand, more pages, custom layouts or integrations |
| WooCommerce store | From ₹50,000 | Brands already on WordPress, or those that want full control of their store |
| Shopify store (template) | From ₹60,000 | New D2C brands that want a stable, hosted store |
| Shopify store (custom) | ₹1.5–5 lakh | Growing brands that have outgrown a template |
| Custom e-commerce development | From ₹2,00,000 | Complex catalogues, B2B pricing or unusual workflows |
| Website maintenance | ₹5,000 / ₹12,000 / ₹25,000 per month | Basic, Business and Store plans for updates, backups and fixes |

A few points on reading this table:

- **Template does not mean generic.** A good template site is styled to your brand, fast on mobile and structured for search. Most small businesses should start here.
- **Custom pays off when the template gets in the way.** If you need unusual layouts, many page types, a product configurator or deep integrations, custom design is the better long-term choice.
- **E-commerce is a different budget.** If you plan to sell online, read our full guide to [ecommerce website cost in India](/blog/ecommerce-website-cost-in-india/) before you decide.

## What drives the price of a website

Two websites with the same page count can differ in price by several times. These are the factors that matter most.

### Number and type of pages
A five-page site with a home, about, services, blog and contact page is quick to build. Fifteen pages with separate service, industry and location pages, each with its own layout, take far more design and content work.

### Custom design or a template
A styled template is the cheapest route. Custom design means wireframes, mock-ups and revision rounds for each key page, which adds time but gives you a site that looks like nobody else's. Our [corporate website development](/corporate-website-development/) work covers both options.

### Content and photos
Content is the most underestimated cost. Who writes the page copy? Do you have good photos of your team, office and work, or will you need a shoot or stock images? Quotes that leave content out look cheaper than they are.

### Integrations
Each connection adds scope: booking tools, payment links, a CRM or lead system, email marketing, live chat, WhatsApp, job applications or an ERP. Ask for each integration to be listed separately in the quote.

### Multilingual pages
Bengaluru businesses often serve customers who prefer Kannada, Hindi or Tamil alongside English. A multilingual site needs translation, a language switcher, and correct technical setup so search engines show the right version. It roughly multiplies content work by the number of languages.

### SEO setup
Basic SEO setup includes page titles, meta descriptions, clean URLs, headings, an XML sitemap, Google Search Console and local business details. This should be part of any professional build. Ongoing SEO is a separate monthly service.

### Speed and mobile performance
A site that loads fast on mobile needs optimised images, lean code and good hosting. It takes more care to build, but a slow site loses visitors and rankings, so this is not the place to cut corners.

## Recurring costs after launch

The design fee is a one-time cost. These costs continue every month or year, and they belong in your budget from day one:

- **Domain name.** Renewed every year. Prices vary by extension (.com, .in, .co.in), so check current prices with your registrar.
- **Hosting.** WordPress and WooCommerce sites need hosting, paid monthly or yearly. Cheap shared hosting is the most common reason sites feel slow. Shopify includes hosting in its plan; check Shopify's current India pricing.
- **Licences.** Premium themes, page builders, form plugins, security tools and backup tools often carry yearly licences.
- **Business email.** Usually a per-user monthly or yearly fee from your email provider.
- **Maintenance.** WordPress core, themes and plugins need regular updates, backups and security checks. Our [website maintenance services](/website-maintenance-services/) start at ₹5,000 per month (Basic), ₹12,000 (Business) and ₹25,000 (Store).

Third-party prices change often and depend on the plan you pick, so ask your developer for a list of every paid service the site will use and check current prices yourself.

## Freelancer or agency?

Bengaluru has a large pool of skilled freelancers and a wide range of agencies, so you will get quotes across a broad range. Neither option is right for everyone.

**A freelancer can suit you if** the site is small and well defined, you can write your own content, and you are comfortable managing the project yourself. The headline price is often lower.

**An agency can suit you if** you need design, development, content, SEO and support from one team, or if the site is important enough that you cannot afford a single point of failure. Agencies usually cost more, but you get more people, a defined process and continuity if someone leaves.

Whichever you choose, ask who will maintain the site after launch, who owns the domain, hosting and code, and what happens if your main contact becomes unavailable.

## Hidden costs to watch for

These are the items that surprise business owners after they sign:

- **Content writing and photography** not included in the design fee.
- **Premium theme or plugin licences** billed to you separately, sometimes every year.
- **Extra revision rounds** beyond the number included in the quote.
- **Stock images** that need a paid licence.
- **Migration from an old site**, including redirects to protect existing search rankings.
- **Post-launch changes** after a short free-support period ends.
- **Hosting upgrades** when the cheap plan in the original quote turns out to be too slow.

## Red flags in website quotes

Be careful when a quote shows any of these signs:

- A single price with no list of pages, features or integrations.
- No mention of who owns the domain, hosting account, theme licence and code.
- No mention of mobile layout, speed or basic SEO setup.
- A very low price paired with a long-term lock-in or a mandatory annual fee.
- Promises of first-page rankings or a fixed number of leads. Nobody controls Google's results.
- The agency registers the domain in its own name instead of yours.

## How to compare website design quotes in Bengaluru

Comparing quotes on the headline number alone rarely works, because each agency scopes different work. Send every vendor the same brief and ask the same questions:

1. How many pages and page layouts are included?
2. Is the design custom or a styled template? How many revision rounds?
3. Who writes the content and sources the photos?
4. Which integrations are included, and which are extra?
5. Which paid themes, plugins or apps will the site need, and who pays for them?
6. What SEO setup is done before launch?
7. What support is included after launch, and for how long?
8. Who owns the domain, hosting, design files and code?

Put the answers side by side in a spreadsheet. This often shows that the cheapest quote leaves out content, licences or support, and that a mid-range quote is better value for the same website design cost in Bangalore.

## How long a website takes

Timelines depend more on preparation than on the build itself. The main factors are:

- **Content readiness.** A site with approved copy and photos moves quickly. Waiting for content is the most common delay.
- **Design rounds.** Each round of feedback adds days. Agree who signs off, and keep the group small.
- **Custom work and integrations.** Custom layouts, multilingual pages and third-party connections need extra development and testing.
- **Access and approvals.** Domain logins, hosting access and legal review of policies can hold up launch if they are not sorted early.

As a rough guide, a template-based WordPress or business site takes a few weeks once content is ready. Custom corporate sites and online stores take longer.
