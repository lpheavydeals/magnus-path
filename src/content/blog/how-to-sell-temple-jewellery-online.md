Working out how to sell temple jewellery online means understanding a craft that is older than ecommerce by centuries. Temple jewellery began as adornment for deities in South Indian temples, and its motifs of Lakshmi, peacocks, lotuses and mangoes, set with red and green kemp stones, now reach Bharatanatyam stages, weddings and festivals around the world. Buyers love it, but they are also wary, because gold and gold-plated pieces can look almost identical in a photo. This guide covers what is specific to temple jewellery. For general jewellery selling, read [how to sell jewellery online](/blog/how-to-sell-jewellery-online/).

## Who buys temple jewellery online

Temple jewellery has four distinct buyer groups, and each needs different pages and messages.

| Buyer | What they look for | What reassures them |
|---|---|---|
| Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi dancers, and their teachers | Complete sets that last through rehearsals and performances | Weight, comfort, adjustability and delivery dates |
| Brides and their families | Heirloom-style sets for the wedding day | Honest material details and a consultation |
| Festive and occasion buyers | Single statement pieces for Navaratri, Deepavali, Pongal and family functions | Photos, reviews and quick delivery |
| Diaspora buyers abroad | Dance and bridal sets they cannot find locally | Shipping times, duties and trust in a distant seller |

Plan your range and collections around these groups, not just around product types.

## Step 1: Describe every material honestly

The most important thing a temple jewellery seller does is say exactly what a piece is made of. Most complaints and returns in this category come from buyers who thought they were getting something else.

### The three common constructions

| Construction | How to describe it | What not to do |
|---|---|---|
| 22 carat gold | "22K gold", with weight, purity and hallmark details | Do not round weights or hide making charges |
| Gold-plated silver | "Gold-plated 92.5 silver" (or the actual purity), with plating type | Do not call it "gold jewellery" |
| Gold-plated brass, copper or alloy | "Gold-plated brass" or "gold-plated alloy" | Do not use "pure gold look" as if it were a material |

Terms such as "one gram gold" and "micro-gold plating" are widely used in the market. If you use them, explain plainly that the piece is plated, what the base metal is and how thick the plating is, if your maker can tell you.

### Stones

Kemp stones are traditionally used in temple jewellery, and many today are synthetic or glass. Rubies, emeralds and pearls in temple designs may be natural, treated, synthetic or imitation. Name what you sell accurately in titles, filters and invoices.

## Step 2: Hallmarking applies to gold only

BIS hallmarking is mandatory for most gold jewellery sold in the districts covered by the rules. Each hallmarked piece carries the BIS mark, the purity grade (such as 22K916) and a six-character HUID that buyers can check. Jewellers selling hallmarked gold need BIS registration, and some light pieces are exempt.

For temple jewellery sellers, the practical rules are:

- Show hallmark and HUID details on every gold product page.
- Never show hallmark images, purity grades or the word "hallmarked" on plated pieces.
- For silver-based pieces, state the silver purity; silver hallmarking is voluntary.
- Keep separate collections and filters for gold and plated pieces, so buyers never confuse them.

Rules on districts and exemptions change, so confirm the current position with your adviser.

## Step 3: Work closely with makers in Nagercoil and Chennai

Nagercoil in Kanyakumari district has a long tradition of handcrafted temple jewellery, and workshops and jewellers in Chennai also make, plate and finish pieces. Many sellers work directly with family workshops.

### What to agree with every maker

- Base metal, silver purity and plating type for each design
- Stone types and how they are set
- Finishing standards: smooth backs, secure hooks, no sharp edges
- Lead times for stock pieces and for custom or bridal orders
- How repairs and re-plating are handled after sale
- Credit for the craft, if the maker is happy to be featured

Visit in person, and check a sample from each new batch before you list it. Handmade pieces vary, so tell buyers that small differences are part of the craft, and show the actual piece where it is one of a kind.

## Step 4: Photography, video and close-ups

Temple jewellery is detailed work, and buyers want to see it up close.

- **Close-ups:** the kemp settings, motifs, the back of the piece, hooks and clasps.
- **On-body shots:** on dancers in costume and on brides in silk, on different skin tones.
- **Scale:** show the piece on a hand or next to a ruler, and list dimensions and weight.
- **Video:** short clips in natural light showing movement and how the stones catch light. Avoid filters that change the gold tone.
- **Full set layouts:** lay out every component of a dance or bridal set so buyers can see what is included.

## Step 5: Build dance set and bridal pages that answer questions

### Dance sets

Dancers and teachers often buy for an arangetram or a season of performances. A good dance set page:

- Lists every component, such as the head set, sun and moon pieces, ear pieces, short and long necklaces, waist belt, bangles and nose ornament
- States total weight and the weight of heavy pieces
- Explains adjustability for head and waist pieces
- Gives care advice for sweat, stage lights and storage
- Shows a firm delivery date and a cut-off for a given performance date

Selling sets and single replacement pieces helps teachers who equip several students.

### Bridal sets

Bridal buyers involve the family and take longer to decide. Offer a WhatsApp or video consultation, show the set worn with a silk saree, and be completely clear whether it is gold or plated. For gold sets, show a price breakup with metal value, making charges and taxes.

## Step 6: Reach diaspora buyers abroad

Tamil and Indian communities in the US, the UK, Singapore, Malaysia, Australia, Canada and the Gulf are an important market for temple jewellery, especially dance schools preparing students for an arangetram.

- Show prices in Indian rupees with a note on currency conversion at checkout.
- State delivery times for each region and who pays import duties and taxes.
- Plan around performance and wedding dates, and give a firm order-by date.
- Offer video calls at times that suit buyers in other time zones.
- Check export and customs rules for jewellery with your courier and adviser, particularly for gold, before you take orders.

Dance teachers abroad often buy for several students at once, so a simple enquiry form for group orders is worth adding.

## Step 7: Insured shipping, payments and returns

### Shipping

- Use couriers that accept jewellery and offer insurance or declared-value cover; check limits before you ship.
- Pack in rigid, padded boxes, with anti-tarnish pouches for plated pieces.
- Use plain, tamper-evident outer packaging with no branding.
- Record a packing video for valuable orders, and require OTP or signature on delivery.

### Payments and COD

For gold, most sellers take prepaid orders or a partial advance. For plated pieces, COD can work if you confirm every order before dispatch. Check what refused parcels cost with our [RTO and COD cost calculator](/tools/rto-cod-calculator/).

### Returns

Set clear terms by material. Many sellers accept returns or exchanges on unworn plated pieces within a short window, offer exchange or buyback on gold based on metal value, and exclude custom pieces. Inspect returns on video and keep records.

## Seasons and content that bring buyers

Temple jewellery demand follows the dance and wedding calendar more than general sale days.

- **The Margazhi music and dance season:** from December into January, Chennai hosts a long season of concerts and dance performances, and dancers buy and refresh sets beforehand.
- **Arangetram season:** debut performances are planned months ahead, in India and abroad, so dance set campaigns should start early.
- **Wedding months:** in Tamil Nadu, the Thai, Aavani and other auspicious months bring bridal and guest demand.
- **Festivals:** Navaratri, Deepavali, Pongal and Varalakshmi Vratham bring single-piece and gifting orders.

Content that works well includes styling videos with dancers, explainers on gold versus gold-plated, care guides for plated pieces, and short stories about how a Nagercoil or Chennai maker sets kemp stones. These answer buyer questions and give search engines useful pages to show.

## How to sell temple jewellery online: a checklist

- Every piece labelled as 22K gold, gold-plated silver or gold-plated alloy
- Stones named accurately in titles, filters and invoices
- Hallmark and HUID details shown on gold pieces only
- Makers in Nagercoil or Chennai visited, standards agreed in writing
- Close-up, on-body and full-set video for hero pieces
- Dance set pages with components, weight and delivery dates
- Bridal consultations offered, with price breakups for gold
- Diaspora shipping times, duties and order-by dates published
- Insured, plain, tamper-evident shipping and clear return terms
