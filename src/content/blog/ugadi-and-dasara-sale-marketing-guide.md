Ugadi sale marketing is one of the first big chances of the year for brands selling to Karnataka, yet many treat it as a single day of discounts. Ugadi opens a festive year that runs through Varamahalakshmi, Ganesha Chaturthi and Mysuru Dasara to Deepavali, with Flipkart and Amazon's biggest sales landing in the middle of it. Brands that plan the whole year together spend less, run out of stock less often and speak to buyers in a way that feels local. This guide covers the calendar, offers, Kannada creative, ad budget phasing, marketplace overlaps and stock and courier planning.

## Karnataka's festive calendar for online brands

Hindu festivals follow the lunar calendar, so dates move each year and regional calendars can differ by a day. Always confirm dates in a local calendar; our [ecommerce sale calendar](/resources/sale-calendar/) lists upcoming dates and when to start ads.

| Festival | Usual timing | What buyers look for |
|---|---|---|
| Ugadi | March or April | New clothes, gold, home and kitchen, electronics, festive food |
| Varamahalakshmi | July or August | Pooja items, silk sarees, gold, gifts for women in the family |
| Ganesha Chaturthi | August or September | Idols, décor, sweets, ethnic wear, electronics |
| Navratri and Mysuru Dasara | September or October | Dolls for the Gombe display, ethnic wear, vehicles, gold, electronics |
| Deepavali | October or November | Gifting, lights, sweets, fashion, home and electronics |

Kannada Rajyotsava on 1 November and Sankranti in January add smaller moments for local pride and harvest-themed offers.

## Ugadi sale marketing: a six-to-eight-week plan

Ugadi marks the new year in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and many families see it as an auspicious day for new purchases. Ugadi 2027 falls around 7 April in most calendars.

### Weeks 8 to 6: offer and stock

- Choose a festival theme that fits your category: a fresh start, a home refresh, new clothes for the new year.
- Build bundles and gift sets rather than relying only on percentage discounts.
- Order packaging and stock early, especially for products made in Karnataka that sell across South India.

### Weeks 5 to 3: creative and pages

- Shoot creative that shows real Ugadi moments: mango leaf torans, rangoli, the bevu-bella mix of neem and jaggery, holige on the table.
- Write Kannada and English versions of ads, landing pages and WhatsApp messages.
- Build a festival landing page with the offer, gift sets, delivery dates and clear terms.

### Weeks 3 to 1: ads and reminders

- Start testing ads two to three weeks before the day, while costs are lower.
- Build remarketing pools from video viewers, visitors and add-to-carts.
- Put most of the budget into the final week, on proven ads only.
- Send reminder messages to past buyers with an order-by date.

### After Ugadi

Keep a small budget running for late buyers, thank customers, ask for reviews and note what sold, which creative won and where stock ran short. Those notes become the starting point for Dasara.

### Offers that suit Ugadi

Ugadi is about new beginnings, so offers that help buyers start fresh tend to land better than a flat discount. A few ideas by category:

- **Fashion and ethnic wear.** New-year outfits for the family, with matched sets and gift wrapping.
- **Home and kitchen.** Home refresh bundles, pooja room essentials and kitchen upgrades.
- **Food brands.** Festive hampers, holige and payasam ingredient kits, and sweets with clear delivery dates.
- **Beauty and personal care.** Festive gift sets and new-routine bundles.
- **Electronics and appliances.** New-year upgrade offers and easy EMI messaging where your payment partner supports it.

Keep offer terms simple and visible on the landing page, the cart and every ad. A buyer who has to read small print to understand a festival offer often leaves.

## Warm-up peaks: Varamahalakshmi and Ganesha Chaturthi

Varamahalakshmi is especially important for pooja items, silk sarees, gold and gifts, and Ganesha Chaturthi brings demand for décor, sweets, idols and ethnic wear. Treat both as useful tests before the main season. Try new creative, offers and audiences here, and carry the winners into Dasara and Deepavali when ad costs are higher.

## Dasara season: planning around the marketplace sales

Mysuru Dasara is Karnataka's most famous festival, running across Navratri and ending on Vijayadashami. It usually falls in September or October, the same window in which Flipkart Big Billion Days and Amazon Great Indian Festival tend to begin. That overlap changes how you plan.

### What the overlap means

- **Higher ad costs.** Marketplace ads and Meta auctions get crowded as national brands spend heavily.
- **Price comparisons.** Buyers compare your store's prices with marketplace deals, so plan both together.
- **Deal deadlines.** Marketplaces take deal submissions weeks before the sale; missing them means missing featured placements.
- **A long run into Deepavali.** The season does not end with Dasara; Deepavali follows within weeks, so pace stock and budget.

### How to plan Dasara campaigns

1. Watch seller notices for the announced sale dates and deal deadlines.
2. Decide which products go into marketplace deals and which stay exclusive to your store, such as gift sets or Dasara bundles.
3. Start creative tests eight weeks before, using what you learned at Ugadi and Ganesha Chaturthi.
4. Build Kannada creative around Dasara traditions, such as the Gombe doll display, Ayudha Puja and family gatherings.
5. Plan two peaks of spend: the marketplace sale days and Vijayadashami.
6. Hold back budget and stock for Deepavali.

## Kannada creative that feels local

Kannada creative often performs well with Karnataka families, especially for festivals rooted in local tradition. A few principles:

- **Use a fluent Kannada writer.** Machine translation sounds stiff and can get festival terms wrong.
- **Show local rituals accurately.** Bevu-bella at Ugadi, Gombe displays at Dasara and Varamahalakshmi pooja details matter to buyers who celebrate them.
- **Run Kannada and English side by side.** Compare results in Bengaluru and in other districts, where Kannada often matters more.
- **Keep offers consistent.** Prices, terms and product names must match in both languages.
- **Mix formats.** Short reels, carousel ads for gift sets and WhatsApp messages for past buyers.

## Phasing your festive ad budget

A festive budget spent in the final three days is mostly spent in the most expensive auctions. Phase it instead:

| Phase | Timing | Share of effort | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test | 6 to 3 weeks before | Small | Find winning creative and offers |
| Build | 3 to 1 weeks before | Growing | Reach new buyers and fill remarketing pools |
| Peak | Final week | Largest | Spend on proven ads and warm audiences |
| Tail | After the festival | Small | Late buyers, reviews and returns |

Check your break-even ROAS before the peak so you know how far you can raise bids. Our [ad budget planner](/tools/ad-budget-planner/) helps you size each phase from your sales and margin targets.

## Quick commerce in festive weeks

In Bengaluru, many festival purchases are made at the last minute: flowers, pooja items, sweets, gifts and party supplies. If your products are on Blinkit, Zepto or Instamart, check stock levels in Bengaluru dark stores before each festival, raise in-app ad bids in the final days, and make sure your packs show clearly in festival searches. Quick commerce does not replace your own store's campaign, but it catches buyers who decide on the day itself.

## Stock and courier planning

- **Forecast by festival.** Use last year's sales or your Ugadi results to estimate Dasara and Deepavali demand.
- **Place stock near buyers.** Fast-moving products in a Bengaluru or Karnataka warehouse reach buyers faster, and quick-commerce stock in Bengaluru serves last-minute orders.
- **Confirm courier capacity.** Ask your courier or aggregator about pickup limits and delays in festive weeks.
- **Show order-by dates.** Put them on product pages, at checkout and in messages, and switch to gift cards once the date passes.
- **Plan returns.** Festive sales bring more returns; have stock and staff ready for exchanges.

## Measuring what worked

Write down results after every festival, not only after Deepavali. Track sales and ad cost by festival, by channel and by language, along with new versus returning buyers, average order value, stock-outs and late deliveries. Compare Kannada and English creative, and Bengaluru with the rest of Karnataka.

These notes make next year's plan faster and cheaper. The creative that won at Ugadi is a strong starting point for Varamahalakshmi; the stock-outs at Dasara tell you what to order more of before Deepavali. Brands that keep this record usually start each season with tested ideas instead of guesses, and spend less of the peak budget on learning.

## Ugadi and Dasara checklist

- Festival dates confirmed in a local calendar
- Offers, bundles and gift sets planned six to eight weeks ahead
- Kannada and English creative written by a fluent Kannada writer
- Marketplace deal deadlines tracked and store pricing planned alongside
- Ad budget split into test, build, peak and tail
- Stock placed near Karnataka buyers and courier capacity confirmed
- Order-by dates on pages and messages
