More UK brands now ask how to sell on TikTok Shop UK, because it puts a buy button inside the videos people already watch. A shopper can see a creator use a product, tap it and check out without leaving the app. For products that look good on camera and are easy to explain in a few seconds, such as beauty, fashion, accessories, homeware and gadgets, it has become a real sales channel alongside Amazon UK and brands' own stores.

It is also a channel with its own rules: approvals, shipping standards, creator commission and UK advertising codes. This guide takes a brand through each step. Platform rules change often, so treat the details as general and check TikTok's current seller policies before you act.

## Step 1: seller onboarding in general terms

Sellers apply through TikTok Shop Seller Center. In general, you will provide:

- Business details, such as a company registration number for limited companies
- Identity documents for the business owner or directors
- A UK bank account for payouts
- A UK warehouse or return address
- VAT details, if registered

Some categories, such as beauty, food and supplements, may need extra approval or documents like safety reports, test results or proof of brand ownership. Some products are restricted or banned. Read the prohibited and restricted product lists before you choose your launch range.

Once approved, link your shop to a TikTok account. Many brands connect their main brand account, so organic videos and shop listings build each other up.

## Step 2: write product listings for video shoppers

TikTok Shop listings are seen on small screens, often after a short video. Keep them clear and quick to scan.

| Listing element | What works |
|---|---|
| Title | Product type, key feature, size or shade, in plain words |
| Main image | Clean product shot that matches what creators show |
| Extra images | In use, scale, texture, pack contents |
| Description | Short benefits, ingredients or materials, care and sizing |
| Variants | Shades or sizes grouped under one listing |
| Price and offers | VAT-inclusive, with bundles where they make sense |
| Stock | Synced with your store so you never oversell |

Claims in listings fall under UK advertising rules as well as TikTok's own policies. Avoid health or medical claims, before-and-after promises and anything you cannot prove.

## Step 3: work with affiliate creators

Affiliate creators are the engine of most TikTok Shop sales. They add your products to their showcase, make videos or go LIVE, and earn a commission you set.

### Finding the right creators

- Open your products to all eligible creators with a standard commission
- Send targeted invitations, with samples, to creators whose audiences match your buyers
- Look for creators who already make videos in your category and get real comments, not only views
- Include London and UK creators whose followers can receive your products quickly

### Briefing creators

Give each creator a short brief: what the product does, who it suits, the main points to show, claims to avoid and how to label the post. Let them speak in their own style. Scripted videos rarely sell as well as honest demonstrations.

### Tracking results

Seller Center shows sales by creator and by video. Each month, increase commission or send new products to the creators who sell, and stop sending samples to those who do not.

## Step 4: make videos that sell

Shop videos differ from brand videos. People scroll quickly, so the first second has to show the product or the problem it solves.

### What tends to work

- **Demonstrations:** show the product doing its job, close up and in real light
- **Unboxing and first use:** honest reactions as a creator opens and tries it
- **Comparisons:** before and after, done fairly and without filters that exaggerate
- **Answering comments:** reply to a viewer's question with a new video
- **Bundles and gifting:** show what is in a set and who it suits

### Building a routine

Post regularly from your own brand account as well as through creators. Batch filming saves time: one afternoon can produce a week of short videos. Use captions on screen, because many people watch without sound. Pin the product link on every shop video and keep the product in stock while the video is getting views.

### Learning from results

Seller Center and TikTok analytics show which videos lead to orders. Look at the opening seconds, the product shown and the creator style of your top sellers, then make more videos like them. Retire formats that get views but no sales.

## Step 5: add LIVE selling

LIVE selling turns a stream into a shop counter. A host shows products, answers questions in real time and pins items for viewers to buy.

1. **Pick a host** who knows the products and is comfortable on camera, from your team or a creator.
2. **Set a schedule**, such as the same evening each week, so viewers know when to return.
3. **Plan a running order** with hero products, demonstrations and a few time-limited offers.
4. **Prepare the set**: good light, clean sound, stock within reach and a second person reading comments.
5. **Review each session** for viewers, orders, products sold and the moments that drove sales.

Start small and stay consistent. Audiences for LIVE often build slowly, then grow once regular viewers start returning.

LIVE sessions also feed your other content. Clip the strongest demonstrations and answers into short videos, and note the questions viewers ask most, because they belong on your product pages and in creator briefs. If no one on the team enjoys hosting, a creator who already sells in your category can host sessions for a fee or commission.

## Step 6: fulfilment and returns

TikTok Shop sets dispatch times and tracks your performance. Late shipments, cancellations and poor reviews can lower your shop's score and limit how often your products are shown.

- Ship yourself with approved couriers such as Royal Mail, Evri or DPD, or use TikTok's fulfilment service where it is available for your products
- Upload tracking promptly for every order
- Keep stock synced across TikTok Shop, your store and any marketplace
- Answer customer messages quickly, including weekends during campaigns

UK consumer rights apply to TikTok Shop sales, so buyers can generally cancel and return within the cancellation period, with exceptions such as opened hygiene-sealed goods. Handle return requests quickly and fairly; slow responses hurt your rating.

## Step 7: stay within ASA and CAP rules

UK advertising rules apply to TikTok content just as they do to TV and print. In general terms:

- Paid, gifted or commission-based creator posts are usually ads and should be clearly labelled, for example with #ad up front
- Claims must be backed by evidence, and health or medicinal claims are tightly restricted
- Filters that exaggerate a product's effect, such as on skin or hair, can make an ad misleading
- Prices, discounts and "was" prices must be genuine
- Brands share responsibility with creators for what is posted

Brief every creator on these points and keep a record of what you asked them to say.

## How to sell on TikTok Shop UK alongside Meta ads and your store

TikTok Shop works well as one part of a wider plan, not as the whole business.

### TikTok Shop ads

Once organic and creator videos prove which products sell, you can pay to show them to more people through TikTok's shop ad formats. Boost videos that already sell rather than making new ones only for ads. Use our [ROAS calculator](/tools/roas-calculator/) to check whether paid campaigns make money after fees and commission.

### Meta ads

The same creator videos often work on Facebook and Instagram, if you have agreed usage rights. Meta ads reach different shoppers and send them to your own store, where you can sell bundles and collect emails. Our [Meta ads agency in London](/london/meta-ads-agency/) plans TikTok and Meta campaigns together, so creative tested on one platform feeds the other.

### Your own store

Your store holds the full range, subscriptions and customer list. Many brands use TikTok Shop for first purchases, then win reorders through email, inserts in the parcel and loyalty offers on their own site.

## Seasonal campaigns on TikTok Shop

TikTok Shop runs its own sales events through the year, and UK shoppers also respond to Black Friday, Christmas, January sales, Valentine's Day and Mother's Day. Plan stock, creator samples and LIVE sessions several weeks ahead of each moment you want to join. Check campaign terms before you sign up, because event discounts and commission come out of the same margin. A clear calendar stops your shop running deep discounts every week.
