If you are researching how to start a skincare brand in Karnataka, the first decision is not your logo or your hero serum. It is how the product will be made legally, because cosmetics manufacturing is licensed state by state. Bengaluru gives you formulation chemists, contract manufacturers, packaging suppliers and a large base of ingredient-aware buyers within one city. This guide walks through the Karnataka-specific steps: choosing a manufacturing route, working with the state drugs control department, finding a manufacturer near Bengaluru, importing, labels, claims and a sensible first launch.

This is general information, not legal or regulatory advice. Rules and procedures change, so confirm each step with a regulatory consultant or the department itself. For the India-wide steps that apply to any beauty brand, such as niche, packaging and branding, see our guide on [how to start a cosmetics brand in India](/blog/how-to-start-a-cosmetics-brand-in-india/).

## Step 1: Choose your manufacturing route

There are four common routes. Each changes what you need from the regulator and how much control you have.

| Route | Whose licence | Suits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Third-party (contract) manufacturer | The manufacturer's | First-time founders testing a small range | Formula ownership, minimum order quantities |
| Loan licence | Yours, at their facility | Brands wanting control over formulas and quality | You carry regulatory responsibility |
| Own manufacturing unit | Yours | Established brands with steady volumes | Premises, staff, equipment, inspections |
| Importing finished products | CDSCO registration | Brands sourcing from abroad | Registration time, stock bought in advance |

Most new skincare brands in Karnataka start with a third-party manufacturer, move to a loan licence once a formula proves itself, and only consider their own unit when volumes justify it.

## Step 2: Work with the Karnataka drugs control department

Under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and the Cosmetics Rules, 2020, the state licensing authority licenses cosmetics manufacturing. In Karnataka, that is the Drugs Control Department of the state government, headquartered in Bengaluru.

### What a manufacturing licence application usually covers

- Details of the premises, layout and the area used for manufacturing and storage.
- Equipment for the product types you plan to make.
- A competent technical person with the qualifications the rules require.
- Arrangements for testing, either in-house or through an approved laboratory.
- The list of products and their categories.

The department may inspect the premises before granting the licence. Applications are usually made online, and the forms and fees are set out in the rules. Check the current portal, documents and timelines with the department before you sign a lease.

### Plan for time, not just paperwork

Licensing is rarely the slowest part on paper, but queries, missing documents and inspection dates can add weeks. Build the licence timeline into your launch plan, and do not book a launch date, influencer campaign or quick-commerce listing until the licence or your manufacturer's coverage is confirmed in writing. Keep copies of every licence, approval and test report in one shared folder, because marketplaces, retailers and investors will ask for them.

### How a loan licence differs

A loan licence is for a brand that wants its own licence but uses another manufacturer's licensed facility. You apply with details of the host facility and your products, and you remain responsible for quality and compliance. The label then shows your licence details. It is a useful middle step between contract manufacturing and building a factory.

### Ayurvedic and herbal products

If your product is sold as Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani rather than as a cosmetic, it follows the AYUSH licensing route instead. Decide early which category your product sits in, because it changes the licence, label and the claims you can make.

## Step 3: Find a third-party manufacturer near Bengaluru

Contract manufacturers for personal care operate in and around Bengaluru's industrial areas, such as Peenya, Bommasandra and Jigani, and many founders also work with makers in neighbouring states. Being close helps with sampling, line visits and quality checks, but the right partner matters more than the shortest drive.

### Questions to ask before you sign

1. Does your cosmetics licence cover this product category, and can we see a copy?
2. Who owns the formula, and can we take it elsewhere later?
3. What are your minimum order quantities for the first batch and for repeat batches?
4. Which stability, preservative efficacy and safety tests do you run, and which do we pay for?
5. Can you source the actives at the strength we want to state on the label?
6. How do you handle batch records, retained samples and complaints?
7. Will you sign a confidentiality agreement before sharing formulas?

### Testing to budget for

Ask your manufacturer which tests they include and which you must commission. Most skincare brands plan for stability testing in the final pack, preservative efficacy testing for water-based products, and safety or patch testing where they intend to mention it. SPF claims need SPF testing at a suitable laboratory. Packaging compatibility matters too: some actives react with certain plastics or degrade in clear bottles, so test in the pack you will actually sell.

Visit the facility, ask for references from other brands, and run a small pilot batch before committing to large volumes.

## Step 4: Importing skincare into Karnataka

If you import finished cosmetics, a state licence is not the route. Imported cosmetics need a registration certificate from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO), the central regulator. The application is filed online by the foreign manufacturer or its authorised importer or agent in India, with product, label and manufacturer documents. Registration applies to named brands and products, so adding new products later can mean new filings.

Imported packs also need Indian label details, such as the importer's name and address and the registration number, often added with a sticker before sale. Plan for the time this takes before you order stock.

## Step 5: Get labels right

Marketplaces and quick-commerce platforms check labels during listing, and a missing field can stall your launch. A skincare label in India typically needs:

- Product name and ingredient list.
- Net quantity and MRP inclusive of taxes.
- Manufacturer's name and address, and licence or registration number.
- Marketer's name and address if different from the manufacturer.
- Batch number, date of manufacture and expiry or use-before date.
- Directions for use and warnings where relevant.
- Consumer care details, as legal metrology rules require.

English labels are standard. Some Karnataka brands add Kannada on an insert or outer sleeve for local shoppers, which is fine as long as mandatory details remain complete and readable.

## Step 6: Keep claims within cosmetic limits

Claims cause more rejected ads and listings than anything else in skincare. A cosmetic can cleanse, moisturise, improve the look of skin, or protect from the sun when tested for SPF. Once a claim says a product treats, prevents or removes a skin condition, it is describing a drug.

- Hold test reports for every claim, including strengths of actives and any "tested" wording.
- Follow the ASCI code for advertising and ASCI's guidelines for influencers, including clear disclosure labels.
- Check Meta and Google policies, which restrict before-and-after images and copy that implies personal attributes.
- Brief creators in writing on what they can and cannot say.

## Step 7: Launch in Bengaluru, then the state

A Bengaluru-first launch lets you learn quickly before spreading budget across Karnataka.

1. **Start with three to five hero products** that form a simple routine.
2. **Sell on your own store first**, with ingredient-led product pages and reviews.
3. **Test quick commerce** for fast-moving items such as sunscreen and cleanser in the city.
4. **Run English, Kanglish and Kannada creative** and compare results by language.
5. **Expand to Mysuru, Mangaluru, Hubballi-Dharwad and Belagavi** with Kannada ads once Bengaluru shows repeat orders.

Offline moments help too. Bengaluru has a steady calendar of flea markets, pop-ups and community events where founders can hand out paid minis, watch how people react to textures and collect honest feedback. Pair every sample with a QR code to a routine guide and a credit towards a full-size product, so you can trace which events bring orders.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Booking a launch before the licence is confirmed.** Listings, creator posts and ads all stall if a marketplace asks for a licence you do not yet hold.
- **Assuming the manufacturer's licence covers everything.** Licences list product categories. A new format, such as a sunscreen or a spray, may need to be added first.
- **Leaving formula ownership vague.** Without a written agreement, moving to a new manufacturer can mean starting the formula again.
- **Writing claims before tests.** Decide what you can prove, then write copy, not the other way round.
- **Launching across Karnataka at once.** Spreading a small budget thinly across cities makes it hard to learn which creative and products work.
