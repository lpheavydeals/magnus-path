If you want to know how to advertise on ChatGPT, the process is shorter than it sounds: open an account in OpenAI Ads Manager, choose an objective, describe what you sell, build a simple ad, install tracking and run a small test. ChatGPT is where many people now research products, compare options and plan purchases, so ads in ChatGPT reach them at a useful moment. This guide walks through each step, explains how pricing works, and shows how ChatGPT ads compare with Google Ads and with organic AI search optimization.

Everything below is accurate as of October 2026. ChatGPT advertising is new and OpenAI changes eligibility, formats and policies often, so check OpenAI's help center before you plan budgets.

## What are OpenAI ads in ChatGPT?

OpenAI ads are paid placements shown inside ChatGPT, below a relevant answer. They are labelled as ads and kept separate from the answer itself. Advertisers cannot pay to change what ChatGPT says, and they never see individual conversations.

Each ad is made up of a few simple parts:

- **Advertiser name and icon**, so people know who is speaking
- **A headline** and **a short description**
- **An image**
- **A link to your landing page**

Because the ad sits next to an answer someone just asked for, it reads more like a suggested next step than a banner. That changes how you should write it, which we cover below.

## Who can advertise on ChatGPT?

OpenAI opened its self-serve Ads Manager to US businesses in May 2026 and has since expanded it to more countries. Eligibility is decided by the country of your billing entity, not where your customers are. A company billed in one country may qualify while a sister company in another does not.

If your billing country is not open yet, you have two options:

1. **Register interest** with OpenAI so you are told when self-serve opens for you.
2. **Contact OpenAI's sales team**, which may work directly with larger advertisers.

Some business categories are restricted or limited whatever your country. Check these before you build anything (see the policy section below).

## How to advertise on ChatGPT, step by step

### Step 1: Create your account in ChatGPT Ads Manager

Go to ads.openai.com and sign up for an advertiser account. You will add your business details, brand name and icon, and a billing method. Use a company email and set up the account in your own business's name, even if an agency will run it for you. That way you keep ownership of the campaigns, the data and the billing relationship.

### Step 2: Choose your campaign objective

Ads Manager offers objectives built around two ways of buying:

- **Reach**, bought on CPM (cost per thousand impressions). Useful for awareness when you want to be seen in relevant conversations.
- **Clicks**, bought on CPC (cost per click). Useful when you want people to visit a landing page.

Conversion-optimized campaigns are also available. They aim for people more likely to buy or enquire, but they are still billed per click. Most e-commerce and lead-generation tests start with clicks or conversion optimization, once tracking is in place.

### Step 3: Set your budget and bids

Set a daily or campaign budget and a bid. Keep the first test small enough that losing it would not hurt, and long enough to gather meaningful data. Decide your success rule before you start, for example "cost per order at or below what we pay on Meta", so you are not tempted to judge on clicks alone.

### Step 4: Describe your business for contextual targeting

This is the biggest difference from other ad platforms. ChatGPT ads use contextual targeting: the system matches your ad to the topic of the conversation. There are no keywords to bid on and no demographic targeting by age, gender or similar traits.

Instead, you describe what you sell, what problems it solves and who it helps. Be specific. "Cold-pressed cooking oils for home cooks who care about purity" gives the system far more to work with than "food products". Your landing page and ad copy also signal relevance, so keep all three consistent.

### Step 5: Build the ad

Fill in each element:

- **Name and icon:** your brand name and a clear logo that reads at small sizes.
- **Headline:** continue the person's train of thought. A headline that answers a likely next question usually fits better than a loud discount.
- **Description:** one or two plain sentences on what makes your product the right choice.
- **Image:** a clean product or use-case image. Avoid heavy text overlays.
- **Landing page:** a page that picks up where the conversation left off.

That landing page matters as much as the ad. Answer the follow-up questions near the top, show proof such as reviews, specifications or quality standards, state the price clearly and give one obvious next step.

### Step 6: Install the OpenAI pixel, Conversions API and UTMs

Set up tracking before you spend anything:

- **OpenAI pixel:** add it to your site, ideally through a tag manager, and fire events for key actions such as add to cart, purchase or lead.
- **Conversions API:** send purchases or leads server-side as well, so fewer events are lost to browser restrictions and ad blockers.
- **UTM tags:** add them to every ad link so your analytics tool shows ChatGPT ad traffic separately from organic ChatGPT referrals.

Test every event with a real visit and a test order or form submission before launch. A campaign without working tracking cannot be judged.

## Ad policies and restricted categories

OpenAI publishes advertising policies that limit or restrict some categories, especially sensitive areas such as health, finance and political content. Policies also cover claims. A supplement or wellness brand, for example, needs to be careful with words like "cure" or "treat", and with before-and-after promises.

Read the current policy pages before writing ads, and check your landing page as well as the ad itself. Policies are still evolving, so something rejected today may be allowed later, and the reverse can also happen.

## What do ChatGPT ads cost?

There is no fixed price list for ChatGPT ads. Pricing works through the bidding models above: you pay per thousand impressions for reach campaigns, or per click for click and conversion-optimized campaigns. What you actually pay depends on your category, how many other advertisers want the same conversation topics, your bids and how relevant your ad is.

Because the platform is new and costs vary widely, we do not quote typical CPC or CPM figures. Be wary of anyone who does without showing their source. The sensible approach is:

1. Start with a small test budget you can afford to lose.
2. Run it for a fixed period with a success rule agreed in advance.
3. Compare cost per order or cost per qualified lead with your Google and Meta campaigns.
4. Scale step by step if ChatGPT holds up, or pause and try a new angle if it does not.

## Measuring results

Ads Manager reports aggregated figures: impressions, clicks, spend, click-through rate, CPC, CPM and conversions. These are useful for day-to-day optimization, but they are not the final word.

Judge ChatGPT ads on the same business numbers you use everywhere else: cost per order, return on ad spend, cost per qualified lead and, where you can, repeat purchase rate. Check your analytics tool and your store or CRM, not only the platform dashboard, because every ad platform tends to credit itself generously. ## ChatGPT ads vs Google Ads vs AI search optimization

These three channels are often confused. Here is how they differ:

| Area | ChatGPT ads | Google Ads | AI search optimization (GEO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where you appear | Below a ChatGPT answer, labelled as an ad | Search results and Google's network | Inside AI answers, as a mention or citation |
| Targeting | Conversation topic (contextual) | Keywords, audiences, demographics | Not targeted; earned through content and reputation |
| How you pay | CPM or CPC bids | Mostly CPC bids | No media cost; you pay for the work |
| Speed | Can start once your account is approved | Can start quickly | Builds over months |
| Maturity | New, changing often | Mature tools and reporting | New, changing often |

Paid ads in ChatGPT put you next to the answer. [AI search optimization](/ai-search-optimization/) aims to get your brand into the answer itself. They are not substitutes, and many brands will want both: ads for quick, measurable tests and organic work for visibility that lasts.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Launching without tracking.** If the pixel and Conversions API are not tested, you cannot tell whether the test made money.
- **Reusing search ad copy.** Keyword-stuffed headlines feel out of place next to a conversational answer.
- **Vague business descriptions.** With no keywords to bid on, a thin description leads to poor matching.
- **Sending traffic to the homepage.** Point each ad to a page that answers the question behind it.
- **Judging on clicks or CTR.** Cheap clicks that never buy are not a win.
- **Ignoring policy until rejection.** Check category and claims rules before you build creative.
- **Spending too much too soon.** A new channel deserves a controlled test, not a large budget on day one.
