Coimbatore makers often ask how to sell pumps online without breaking the dealer network that built their business. It is a fair worry. The city is known across India for pumps and motors, and most of that trade still runs through dealers, plumbers, electricians and distributors. Buyers have changed, though. A farmer checks a borewell pump on YouTube before visiting a shop, a plumber compares models on his phone, and a dealer in another state finds new brands through Google and IndiaMART. This guide covers buyer types, B2B and B2C channels, spec-led listings, BIS marks, warranty and service, search ads and SEO, and dealer enquiries.

We are an ecommerce and B2B marketing team, not engineers or regulatory advisers. Confirm BIS, labelling and electrical safety points with a qualified adviser.

## Who buys pumps and motors online

Pump buyers are not one audience. Each group searches, compares and buys differently.

| Buyer | What they buy | What they need online |
|---|---|---|
| Households | Self-priming, pressure booster and small openwell pumps | Which model suits their home, easy checkout, installation help |
| Farmers | Openwell, borewell submersible and monoblock pumps | Plain Tamil explanations, head and depth guidance, service nearby |
| Plumbers and electricians | Pumps for client homes and buildings | Fast spec lookup, stock, trade pricing on request |
| Builders and contractors | Pumps for apartments, sumps and projects | Datasheets, bulk quotes, delivery schedules |
| Dealers and distributors | Range for resale | Dealer terms, margins, marketing support, enquiry route |

Your website and listings should serve all of them, but not on the same page. A household page and a dealer page answer different questions.

## B2B or B2C: you probably need both

Most Coimbatore pump makers end up with two tracks.

- **B2C (direct to the end user)**: domestic pumps sold on Amazon, Flipkart and your own store, where buyers can choose without an engineer.
- **B2B (trade and projects)**: larger agricultural pumps, borewell sets and industrial motors sold through enquiries, dealers, IndiaMART and your own website.

The mistake is forcing one track to do the other's job. A 7.5 HP borewell submersible rarely sells through a cart, because the buyer needs advice on depth, cable, panel and installation. A small booster pump for a two-storey house can.

## How to sell pumps online with spec-led listings

Pump buyers search by specification. Your pages have to answer in the same language, then translate it for buyers who are not engineers.

### What every pump page needs

1. **A plain line on who it suits.** "For a two-storey house with an overhead tank" or "for borewells up to a stated depth with a stated casing size".
2. **A full spec table.** Head range, flow at different heads, power in HP and kW, phase and voltage, outlet and pipe size, borewell size, number of stages, materials, duty type and cable length if supplied.
3. **A performance curve or table.** Flow at each head, so plumbers and farmers can check the duty point.
4. **Installation needs.** Panel or starter, cable, pipe, foot valve, what is in the box and what is not.
5. **Warranty and service.** Length, terms, how to claim, and where service is available.
6. **A downloadable datasheet** for plumbers, contractors and dealers.
7. **Videos.** Unboxing, set-up and the pump running, filmed at your unit or on a real site.

### Add a pump selector

A simple selector asks three or four questions, such as water source, depth, number of floors or acres, and power supply, then shows two or three suitable models. It reduces wrong orders, returns and calls asking "which one should I buy?".

### Keep model names identical everywhere

Use the same model numbers on your website, Amazon, Flipkart, IndiaMART, datasheets and dealer price lists. Buyers and search engines both need to match them.

## BIS marks, labels and compliance

Many pumps and motors sold in India fall under Indian Standards, and some types are covered by quality control orders that make BIS certification compulsory. The ISI mark tells buyers a product is made under a BIS licence. Agricultural pumpsets may also carry an energy star label under the Bureau of Energy Efficiency scheme, where it applies.

How to handle this online:

- Show the ISI mark and licence details only for models your licence covers.
- Explain in one line what the mark means, without overstating it.
- Keep scanned licences ready, because marketplaces may ask for them.
- Follow legal metrology and e-commerce rules on seller details, country of origin, warranty and returns.

Confirm what applies to each model with a qualified adviser. The lists change from time to time.

## Warranty and service network

For pump buyers, the question behind every purchase is "what happens when it fails?". A farmer who cannot water a crop for a week will not buy from a brand with no visible service.

- Put warranty terms on every product page, not only in the manual.
- Publish a service and dealer locator by district.
- Give a WhatsApp number for service requests, and reply in Tamil and English.
- Ask buyers to register their pump, so you can remind them about servicing.
- Answer marketplace reviews that mention service quickly and publicly.

If your service network is strongest in Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Karnataka, say so, and focus ads on those states first.

## IndiaMART vs Amazon vs your own site

| Channel | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| IndiaMART and similar B2B portals | Trade and project enquiries, dealers in other states | Shared leads, price-only buyers, variable lead quality |
| Amazon and Flipkart | Domestic pumps buyers choose themselves | Heavy-item shipping, returns, price comparison |
| Your own website | Brand, spec searches, dealer enquiries, datasheets | Needs SEO and ads to bring traffic |
| WhatsApp Business | Plumbers, dealers, service requests | Needs a person to answer quickly |

Track which channel brings orders, not just enquiries. A simple sheet that marks each enquiry as quoted, won or lost tells you more than any lead count. ## Google Search ads and SEO for spec queries

Pump buyers type very specific searches. That is good news, because specific searches show clear intent.

### SEO

- Build a page for each model and each main application, such as openwell, borewell, domestic booster or sewage.
- Use spec terms in titles and headings: HP, phase, head and borewell size.
- Write Tamil and English guides on choosing a borewell pump or sizing a booster for an apartment, with every Tamil line written or checked by a fluent Tamil writer.
- Add dealer and service pages by district for local searches.

Our [SEO services in Coimbatore](/coimbatore/seo-services/) page explains how we approach this for manufacturers.

### Google Search ads

- Bid on exact spec and model terms, not broad words like "pump".
- Send each ad group to the matching model or selector page, not the home page.
- Run separate campaigns for households, farmers and dealers, with different landing pages.
- Use call and WhatsApp extensions, because many pump buyers prefer to talk.
- Plan budgets around summer, when wells and sumps run low, and around sowing seasons in your main districts.

## Videos that answer the counter questions

At a dealer's counter, a buyer asks how loud the pump is, how it is fitted and what happens when the power trips. Online, short videos answer those questions. Film a set-up clip for each main model, a running clip that shows the delivery pipe filling a tank or channel, and a short explainer on choosing the right head and HP. Shoot at your unit and on a real farm or building site where the owner agrees. Tamil versions suit farmers and plumbers in the region; English versions reach dealers and buyers in other states. Use the same clips on product pages, YouTube, Amazon listings and dealer WhatsApp groups, so one shoot serves every channel.

## Turning dealer enquiries into a channel

Your website should recruit dealers as well as sell pumps.

1. Add a "become a dealer" page with the regions you want to cover, what you offer and a short form.
2. Ask for the business name, location, current brands and showroom details, so you can qualify leads.
3. Reply within a working day, ideally by phone or WhatsApp.
4. Send consumer enquiries from a dealer's area to that dealer, and tell the dealer you did.
5. Share product videos, spec cards and Tamil and English social posts dealers can use.
