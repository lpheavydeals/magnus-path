# Homepage Plan (SEO-led)

**Goal:** rank for *ecommerce marketing agency* (+ *india*), act as the hub that passes authority to every
service, location and industry page, and turn visitors into audit requests.
Design language and motion come from the Roveia reference (`docs/reference-roveia.md`). The structure
below is our own, built around search intent.

| # | Section | SEO / conversion job | Motion (Roveia-inspired) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero:** H1 "E-commerce Marketing Agency for Brands That Want to Sell More Online" + intro paragraph with primary keywords + 2 CTAs | Primary keyword in H1, title and first 100 words; LCP is text | Word-by-word blur reveal; an orange media panel with a 3D "path" logo **expands as you scroll** |
| 2 | **Platform strip:** Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Meta, Google, AI search | Entity relevance (Google connects us with these platforms) | Slow marquee |
| 3 | **What we do:** 8 service cards, each with keyword anchor text to its page | Internal links to all pillar and service pages; services listed in schema | Sideways scroll driven by vertical scroll (desktop); swipe on mobile; orange 3D icons |
| 4 | **Who we work with:** Fashion, Beauty & Ayurveda, Food & FMCG, Manufacturers & Exporters | Internal links to industry pages; captures B2B/exporter intent | Pinned list; active item turns orange; detail card swaps |
| 5 | **Why one team:** statement + 4 commitments | Differentiation; E-E-A-T | Words light up as you scroll |
| 6 | **How it works:** 4 steps | Reduces friction; HowTo-style content | Dark panel, steps reveal, floating 3D objects with parallax |
| 7 | **Case studies:** stacking cards | Proof; links to case-study pages | Sticky stacking cards |
| 8 | **Where we work:** Chennai, Coimbatore, Bangalore + UK, UAE, Australia, USA | Local SEO signals; links to Chennai pages; NAP consistency | Fade-up chips |
| 9 | **We don't work with every brand:** qualification list | Lead quality; honest positioning (helps conversion and trust) | Fade-up |
| 10 | **Guides:** 3 cards linking to cornerstone articles | Topical authority; internal links to blog | Fade-up cards |
| 11 | **FAQ:** 8 questions | FAQPage schema; AI-answer eligibility; long-tail keywords (cost, timeline, Chennai) | Accordion |
| 12 | **Final CTA + audit form** | Lead capture | Animated orange wave gradient |
| 13 | **Footer:** NAP + full link map | Crawl paths, local SEO | — |

Target length: ~1,500 words of visible copy. Every heading uses natural keyword variants without stuffing.
