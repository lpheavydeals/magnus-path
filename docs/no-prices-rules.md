# No prices on the website (18 Oct 2026, overrides every earlier price rule)

The client removed all pricing from the site. /pricing/ is deleted. Rules for every page, guide, FAQ, figure and meta tag:

1. Remove every price Magnus Path charges, in any currency (₹, US$, $, £, AED, A$): "from ₹30,000 / month", "starts at
   US$350", "₹1.5–5 lakh", fee percentages such as "10–15% of ad spend", tier prices, "custom quote above X", etc.
2. Also remove general market price ranges for agency/website/marketing services ("agencies typically charge ₹25,000 to
   ₹1,50,000"). The three cost guides must contain no currency figures at all.
3. Remove claims about our pricing style that no longer make sense: "clear starting prices", "published prices",
   "transparent pricing", "see our pricing", "price list". "A fixed written quote before work starts" is fine.
4. Replace with the quote process: we quote after a free audit or a short scoping call; the written quote lists scope,
   timeline and exclusions; for ads, ad spend is paid directly to the platform from the client's account and the
   management fee is quoted separately. Explain what drives the cost (scope, pages, catalogue size, channels, competition,
   integrations, content).
5. Keep "How much does X cost?" FAQs (people search them) but answer with cost drivers + how we quote, no figures.
6. Hero facts that showed a price: replace with a true non-price fact (e.g. "Free audit first", "Fixed written quote",
   "Accounts stay in your name").
7. Figures (diagrams) that show prices: rewrite those labels, keeping the figure-guide length limits.
8. Related links to /pricing/: replace with another relevant allowed URL (keep at least 3 related links).
9. Keep untouched: product/marketplace "pricing" (e.g. Amazon listing pricing), platform fees and ad-auction facts in
   guides (Amazon referral fees, CPC bidding), client sales ranges in the lead form, ROAS calculator examples,
   "Qualify" sales thresholds.
10. All other content-brief rules still apply (banned words, exact keyword in intro, guide >= 700 words, 6+ FAQs,
   description 120-165 chars, title <= 65 chars).
