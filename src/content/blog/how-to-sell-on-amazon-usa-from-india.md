Learning how to sell on Amazon USA from India starts with one program: Amazon Global Selling, which lets Indian businesses list on the US marketplace and reach American buyers. The process is well established, but it is more than a registration form. You need export documents, US-compliant products, a fulfilment and shipping plan, pricing that survives all the fees, and listings written for US shoppers. This guide covers each step in order, so you can plan before you spend money on inventory.

## What Amazon Global Selling is

Amazon Global Selling is Amazon's program for sellers who want to sell on its marketplaces outside their home country. Indian sellers use it to register on Amazon.com and manage the US store through Seller Central. Amazon also offers onboarding help, educational content and access to service providers for logistics, compliance and account setup.

The program does not do the export work for you. You remain responsible for product compliance, shipping, customs and taxes. Think of it as access to the marketplace, with support around the edges.

## Documents you need

Before registering for Amazon Global Selling from India, gather these:

| Requirement | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Identity proof (usually a passport) | Seller identity verification |
| Business details and address | Account registration and invoicing |
| GST registration (if applicable) | Indian tax compliance and export invoicing |
| Importer Exporter Code (IEC) | Required for commercial exports from India |
| Bank account | Receiving payouts from Amazon |
| International credit or debit card | Paying Amazon's plan and service fees |
| Phone number and email | Account security and verification |

Amazon may ask for extra verification, including a video call. Register with the same details that appear on your documents, because mismatches are a common cause of delays. Payouts can be received in an Indian bank account through Amazon's currency conversion service or through a payment service provider; compare the conversion costs before you choose.

On the tax side, exporters often file a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) to export without paying IGST upfront. Ask your chartered accountant to set this up and to advise on any US tax registrations your model requires.

## Research product compliance first

This is the step most sellers skip, and it is the one that causes listings to be blocked or shipments to be held. The US regulates many categories closely:

- **Food, supplements and cosmetics** may fall under FDA rules, including facility registration and labelling requirements.
- **Children's products and toys** usually need safety testing and certificates.
- **Electronics** may need FCC compliance.
- **Textiles and apparel** need correct fibre content and country-of-origin labels.
- **Some states** have their own warning-label rules for certain substances.

Amazon also has category approvals and restricted products of its own. Before you choose products, check Amazon's category requirements in Seller Central and the relevant US agency rules. When in doubt, speak to a compliance consultant. It is cheaper than a stuck shipment.

For Indian exporters, categories such as home textiles, apparel, home decor, handicrafts, leather goods and kitchenware are often easier starting points than food or cosmetics, because the compliance path is simpler.

## FBA vs merchant fulfilled

You have two main ways to deliver orders to US buyers.

### Fulfilled by Amazon (FBA)

You send inventory in bulk to Amazon's US fulfilment centres. Amazon stores, picks, packs, ships and handles returns and customer service. Your products become eligible for Prime delivery, which matters a lot to US shoppers. Amazon FBA from India is the route most serious sellers take.

The costs are fulfilment fees per unit, monthly storage fees and possible long-term storage charges. Slow-moving stock gets expensive, so send small, tested quantities first.

### Merchant fulfilled (FBM)

You ship each order directly from India. This avoids storage fees and upfront inventory risk, but delivery is slower and you must meet Amazon's shipping performance standards. FBM suits light, high-value, personalised or made-to-order items where buyers accept a longer wait.

Many sellers start with a mix: FBA for proven best-sellers, FBM to test new products.

## Logistics and customs

For FBA, your goods travel by sea or air freight to the US, clear customs and are delivered to Amazon's warehouse. Key points:

- **Freight forwarder:** choose one experienced with Amazon FBA deliveries, since Amazon has strict appointment, labelling and packaging rules.
- **Importer of record:** someone must be the importer of record in the US and pay duties. Amazon does not act as your importer. Your forwarder or a customs broker can explain the options.
- **Customs bond and broker:** usually needed for commercial imports into the US.
- **Duties and tariffs:** US duty rates depend on product classification and can change with trade policy. Check the current rate for your HS code before pricing.
- **Labels and packaging:** follow Amazon's FBA labelling and carton rules exactly, or stock may be refused or delayed.

Air freight is faster and better for a first test shipment. Sea freight is cheaper per unit once you know what sells.

## Pricing and fees

Price for the US market after every cost is counted. Your landed cost per unit should include:

1. Product cost and packaging
2. Freight to the US
3. Customs duty, brokerage and importer charges
4. Inbound shipping to Amazon's warehouse
5. Amazon's referral fee on each sale
6. FBA fulfilment and storage fees
7. Your monthly selling plan fee spread across units
8. Advertising cost per unit
9. Returns and currency conversion

Amazon's referral and FBA fees vary by category, size and weight, and they change regularly. Do not rely on rates quoted in old articles. Check the current US fee schedule in Seller Central and use a [marketplace profit calculator](/tools/marketplace-profit-calculator/) to test each product before you order stock. If the numbers only work at a price far above competing listings, pick a different product.

## Listing for US buyers

US shoppers search, read and decide differently from Indian shoppers. Write for them:

- **Titles:** brand, product type, key feature, size or quantity, in plain American English. "Cotton Throw Blanket 50x60 Inches" not "Premium Quality Super Soft Blanket".
- **Units and spelling:** use inches, pounds and US spelling in listings, even though we use Indian English everywhere else.
- **Bullet points:** lead with benefits, then specifics: material, dimensions, care, what is in the box.
- **Images:** a pure white background main image, then lifestyle shots, size references and infographics.
- **A+ Content:** available once you are brand registered; use it to tell the craft or origin story.
- **Backend search terms:** add synonyms and US terms buyers use.

## Sponsored Products in the US

New listings have no sales history, so they rarely appear in organic results. Sponsored Products ads are the fastest way to get the first sales and reviews moving.

Start with an automatic campaign to discover search terms, then build manual campaigns around terms that convert. Add negative keywords to stop waste. Set a daily budget you can sustain for several weeks, because early data is noisy. The US marketplace is competitive, so expect to spend on ads while you build reviews.

## Brand Registry and trademark basics

Amazon Brand Registry gives you more control over your listings, access to A+ Content, Sponsored Brands ads, brand analytics and tools to report counterfeits or listing hijacks.

To enrol, you need a registered or pending trademark from an intellectual property office Amazon accepts. Many Indian brands selling in the US file a US trademark. Amazon has also run programs that connect sellers with vetted IP law firms to speed up access. Check Amazon's current Brand Registry requirements, and work with a trademark attorney so your mark is distinctive enough to be granted.

Apply early. Trademark processes take time, and you want protection before your listing starts getting attention.
