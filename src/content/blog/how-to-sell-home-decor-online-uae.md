Learning how to sell home decor online UAE households will trust means planning for homes that change often and are furnished in a hurry. Many residents move every year or two, new villa communities hand over keys in batches, and families host large gatherings during Ramadan and Eid. Shoppers expect quick delivery, clear assembly promises and the option to pay on delivery or in instalments. This guide covers the decisions that matter most, from the first product photo to the returned parcel.

Our expertise is selling online, not law. Where rules come up below, they are described in general terms. Check your own position with a qualified adviser before you publish policies or import new ranges.

## Pick products that work online

Not every beautiful piece sells well through a screen. Before you photograph a range, score each product on four points:

- **Can it be photographed honestly?** Texture, colour and scale should come across on a phone.
- **Will it survive the journey?** Glass, ceramics and marble need tested packaging.
- **Can it be returned without losing money?** Large or made-to-order pieces need different terms.
- **Does the margin hold after delivery, assembly and damage?** If not, raise the basket size or change the product.

Categories that tend to suit UAE buyers include cushions and throws, rugs, lighting, mirrors, wall art, tableware and serving sets, lanterns, incense and oud burners, and storage. Majlis seating, dining sets and outdoor furniture can be strong sellers too, but they need more work on delivery and assembly.

## Show products in rooms that look like UAE homes

A sofa shot in a cramped European flat tells a Dubai buyer very little. Shoot in settings your customers recognise:

- **Apartments** with floor-to-ceiling windows, open-plan living and compact balconies.
- **Villas** with large living rooms, double-height entrances and family dining for ten.
- **Majlis rooms** with floor or low seating arranged around the walls, rugs and side tables for coffee and dates.
- **Outdoor spaces** such as terraces and gardens, used mainly in the cooler months.

### The photo set to aim for

- A clean cut-out on a plain background for marketplaces.
- A room shot that shows the piece in use.
- A size reference, such as someone sitting on the sofa or a coffee cup beside the vase.
- Close-ups of fabric, grain and finish.
- A short video walking around the piece, filmed in daylight.

Light matters. Strong sun can wash out colours, so show fabrics both in daylight and under warm evening lighting, which is how many people will see them.

## Give exact sizes, materials and access details

Buyers in towers worry about lifts and corridors, and villa owners worry whether a dining table will seat the whole family. Answer both on the page.

| Detail | What to include |
|---|---|
| Dimensions | Height, width, depth, seat height and weight |
| Packed size | Carton measurements, and whether it fits a standard tower lift |
| Materials | Solid wood, veneer, metal, fabric make-up and finish |
| Heat and sun | How the material copes with sun, heat and air conditioning |
| Humidity | Advice for coastal homes and outdoor use |
| Care | Cleaning, fabric care and how to protect surfaces |
| Assembly | Whether needed, who does it and how long it takes |

Lamps and other electrical pieces may need to pass a national conformity process overseen by MOIAT, the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology, and packaged consumer goods are normally expected to show Arabic on the label. Confirm what applies to your range with your supplier or an adviser before you import.

## Plan delivery and assembly properly

Delivery is often where home decor brands win or lose a customer in the UAE.

### Small and fragile items

Use packaging that has been drop-tested, and choose couriers such as Aramex or marketplace fulfilment for parcels that need to arrive quickly. Fragile items deserve their own box inside the outer carton.

### Large and bulky items

Two-person delivery with a booked time slot is the norm for furniture. Many residential towers ask for deliveries to be booked with building management in advance, and some need extra paperwork for large items, so tell customers early and give them the details they need. Offer assembly as a clear option, with the price or the inclusion stated on the product page. Large retailers such as IKEA and Home Centre have shaped what people expect here, so be specific about what you offer and when.

### Coverage

State which emirates you deliver to and how long each takes. Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi buyers often expect fast delivery for small items, while furniture can take longer as long as you say so up front. A WhatsApp message the day before delivery reduces missed slots.

## Write a clear returns policy

Returns can wipe out the margin on a large piece. Write a policy that covers:

- Change-of-mind returns, with a time window and who pays for collection.
- Damaged or faulty deliveries, with a short window to report them and a request for photos.
- Made-to-order or customised pieces, which usually have different terms.
- Opened textiles such as bedding and cushions.
- How refunds work for cash on delivery orders.

UAE consumer protection rules expect clear terms for returns and exchanges. Show your policy before checkout, not only in the footer.

## Build trust with reviews and customer photos

Home decor is a visual, considered purchase, and many UAE shoppers have been let down by a rug that looked richer online or a sofa that arrived in the wrong shade. Reviews with photos from real homes do more to settle those doubts than any studio shot.

- Send the review request about ten days after the delivery team leaves, with a prompt to upload a picture of the room.
- Ask specific questions: did the colour match, how was the delivery team, was assembly easy?
- Show reviews in the language they were written in, and let shoppers filter by Arabic or English.
- Reply to negative reviews calmly and say what you changed.
- With permission, reuse customer photos on product pages and in ads.

Delivery and assembly reviews deserve as much attention as product reviews. If a courier or fitting team keeps getting poor feedback, change the partner before it damages the brand.

## Price for delivery, payment and basket size

Show AED prices including 5% VAT, and put delivery and assembly charges on the product page so nobody is surprised at checkout. Pitch the free delivery level slightly higher than what most people spend, and many will add a throw or a candle holder to reach it. To test different levels against your margins, try our [free shipping threshold calculator](/tools/free-shipping-threshold-calculator/).

Paying the driver remains popular, yet a sofa that is turned away at the villa gate has to travel back at your expense. Many stores offer COD on small items and ask for prepayment or a deposit on large or made-to-order pieces. Tabby and Tamara instalments make big purchases easier to commit to.

## Choose your UAE sales channels

### Your own Shopify store

Your store carries full room sets, delivery booking, assembly options and your returns policy in your own words. Build Arabic and English versions with a proper right-to-left layout, and add WhatsApp for questions about sizes and fabrics. For how we plan this for decor and furniture brands, see our [home decor marketing agency in Dubai](/uae/home-decor-marketing-agency-dubai/) page.

### Amazon.ae and Noon

Both suit smaller pieces such as cushions, frames, rugs, lamps and storage. You have less control over delivery and returns, so many brands keep large furniture on their own store. Keep AED prices consistent across channels.

### Quick commerce and other routes

Talabat and Careem quick commerce are mainly for groceries and everyday items, but candles, home fragrance and small hosting essentials sometimes fit if your distributor can reach them. Namshi is mostly fashion and beauty, so check whether its home range is open to your category before planning around it.

## Market with room ideas, not just products

Instagram is where many UAE buyers collect ideas for a new home. Snapchat and TikTok suit short room tours and before-and-after styling of a rented apartment. Google Search and Shopping catch people searching for a specific piece, in Arabic and English. Create content around real situations: furnishing a first apartment, setting up a majlis, preparing a dining room for iftar or styling a terrace for winter evenings.

## Plan around the UAE home calendar

| Period | What happens | What to prepare |
|---|---|---|
| Before Ramadan | Living and dining rooms refreshed for iftar hosting | Serving ware, lanterns, table linen and majlis pieces |
| Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha | Family gatherings and gifting | Gift sets, delivery cut-offs, extra seating |
| Summer | Lease renewals and moves, heat, many residents away | Moving bundles, bedroom and storage, delivery capacity |
| Late August | Back to school | Study desks, kids' rooms, storage |
| October to March | Outdoor season, new villa handovers | Outdoor furniture, full-room packages |
| November to January | White Friday, UAE National Day, Dubai Shopping Festival | Stock, offers and creative booked early |

Ramadan moves about eleven days earlier each year, so set your stock dates against the current year's calendar.

## How to sell home decor online UAE: launch checklist

- [ ] Products scored for photography, shipping, returns and margin.
- [ ] Every lead product has a white-background image, a styled room shot, a size reference, detail shots and a short video.
- [ ] Sizes, packed dimensions, materials, heat, humidity and care advice on every page.
- [ ] Conformity and Arabic labelling checked for relevant items.
- [ ] Delivery coverage, slots, building access notes and assembly options published.
- [ ] Returns policy shown before checkout, including made-to-order terms.
- [ ] COD rules, deposits and Tabby or Tamara set up.
- [ ] Arabic and English store versions with WhatsApp support.
- [ ] Seasonal calendar with stock and creative dates.

## Growing beyond the UAE

Saudi Arabia and the rest of the GCC are natural next steps for many decor brands, with similar hosting traditions and larger homes. Delivery, assembly, returns and labelling all need fresh planning for each country, so prove your model in the UAE first.
