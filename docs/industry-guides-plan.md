# Industry guides (blog) — 24 informational guides supporting the industry pages

Two guides per industry: a "how to sell <category> online" guide and a "<category> marketing ideas/strategy" guide.
Format: Markdown in `src/content/blog/<slug>.md`, frontmatter exactly as the existing posts (see
`how-to-reduce-rto-cod-returns.md`), `category: "Industries"`, `author: "Billion Ecommerce Agency Team"`,
`publishDate: 2026-10-25`. India-first unless the slug says otherwise. 1,600–2,200 words of body, H2/H3 structure,
a short answer-first `summary` (60–90 words), 5–6 FAQs, 1–2 figures (same shapes as existing posts), 5–7 related links
that must include the India industry page and its Chennai and Bangalore versions.
