If you are working out how to start a D2C brand in Coimbatore, you probably already make something. The Kongu region, from Coimbatore and Tiruppur to Erode, Karur and Pollachi, is full of units that supply other brands: pumps and motors, wet grinders, knitwear, home textiles, coconut products, spices and engineering parts. Many owners now want their own label, sold straight to the people who use it. This guide covers that move, from choosing the first product to registration, fulfilment, couriers, COD and creative in Kongu Tamil and English.

We are an ecommerce growth team, not chartered accountants or lawyers. Treat the registration points as general guidance and confirm them with your adviser.

## Why Kongu region makers are building their own brands

Selling to dealers, distributors and overseas buyers keeps a factory busy, but it also means someone else owns the customer, the retail margin and the brand name. A D2C brand changes that:

- **You set the retail price**, rather than accepting the buyer's margin squeeze.
- **You hear from users directly**, through reviews, WhatsApp chats and returns, which helps product decisions.
- **You build an asset**, a name buyers search for, which a supplier relationship never becomes.

The trade-off is new work you may not have done before: product pages, photography, ads, customer service and handling many small parcels instead of a few large consignments. If you also export garments, our guide on [how Tiruppur garment exporters sell online](/blog/how-tiruppur-garment-exporters-can-sell-online/) goes deeper on that specific move.

## Step 1: Pick one hero product from what you already make

Do not try to put your whole catalogue online. Choose one or two products that meet three tests:

1. **Easy to explain in a photo and a short video.** A wet grinder, a bedsheet set or a bottle of cold-pressed oil works. A custom industrial part usually does not.
2. **Ships safely by courier.** Check weight, fragility and leakage before you commit.
3. **Has a margin that survives retail costs.** Shipping, COD charges, returns, marketplace fees and ads all come out of the price.

Test the hero with people you can reach: family WhatsApp groups, your workers' networks, local exhibitions at the Codissia trade fair grounds, weekend stalls and Instagram. Ask strangers, not just friends, to buy. Write down every question they ask before ordering; those questions become your product page.

## Step 2: Keep the brand separate from the B2B business

This is the step most Coimbatore makers skip, and it causes the most friction later.

| Area | Keep separate because |
|---|---|
| Brand name and packaging | Dealers may object if your retail label competes with theirs on the same shelf |
| Pricing | Selling online below your trade price can upset distributors |
| Marketplace and store accounts | Mixing B2B invoices with retail orders confuses stock and tax records |
| Team owner | Online orders need daily attention that factory staff rarely have |
| Stock | Retail stock should be counted apart so a big B2B order does not empty it |

Talk to your key dealers before launch. Some will welcome a brand that builds awareness for the category; others will want reassurance that you will not undercut them.

## Step 3: Registration, GST and licences

### Structure

Many founders run the brand from their existing proprietorship, partnership or company. Others create a new LLP or private limited company for it, especially if they plan to bring in a partner or investor. Your chartered accountant can compare the tax and compliance effect of each.

### The usual checklist

1. PAN and a current account in the name of the selling entity
2. GST registration, or a check that your existing GST covers retail sales and every state you will store stock in
3. Trademark application for the brand name and logo, filed before you spend on packaging and ads
4. Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments registration if you have an office, store or godown
5. Udyam registration if you want to use MSME schemes
6. Product-specific approvals: FSSAI for food and edible oils, the cosmetics rules for personal care, and BIS or other standards marks for some electrical appliances
7. Legal Metrology declarations on every retail pack: net quantity, manufacturer or marketer address, date of manufacture or import, MRP and consumer care details

Rules change and differ by product, so check the full list with your adviser.

## Step 4: Warehousing and fulfilment in western Tamil Nadu

### Start inside your own unit

Most Coimbatore makers have space. A clean, dry corner with racks, a packing table, a weighing scale and a label printer is enough for the first months. Assign one person to packing so quality stays consistent.

### When to move to a godown or 3PL

Move when packing slows production or when order volume makes daily stock counts hard. Warehousing around Coimbatore is spread along the Avinashi Road, Trichy Road and L&T bypass belts and in industrial areas such as Kurichi and Chettipalayam. Choose a place couriers already visit daily, with good road access and secure storage.

Once you sell on Amazon or Flipkart, compare packing yourself with sending stock to their fulfilment centres. Fulfilment programmes can help with delivery speed and badges, but they add storage and handling fees, so check the numbers for your product.

## Step 5: Couriers, COD and returns

### Couriers

A courier aggregator gives access to several national couriers through one dashboard, so you can choose the courier that serves each pin code well. Check delivery to the towns your buyers live in, from Tiruppur and Erode to the Nilgiris and Kerala border areas, not only Coimbatore city.

### Cash on delivery

COD is still common across Tamil Nadu, especially for a brand buyers have not heard of. It also brings refused parcels and return-to-origin (RTO) costs.

- Confirm every COD order on WhatsApp before dispatch, in Tamil or English.
- Verify the address and phone number; hold orders that look odd.
- Offer a small prepaid benefit, such as faster dispatch.
- Track RTO by pin code, product and ad campaign.

Run your own numbers through our [RTO and COD cost calculator](/tools/rto-cod-calculator/) to see what refused orders really cost, and read our guide to [reducing RTO and COD returns](/blog/how-to-reduce-rto-cod-returns/).

### Returns

Write a returns policy that a buyer understands in one read: what can be returned, within how many days, who pays the return shipping and how refunds are made. For appliances, explain service and warranty separately from returns.

## Step 6: Build a store and creative that sound local

### The store

Your own store holds your story, pricing and customer data. Shopify suits most new brands; WooCommerce works if you already run WordPress. Include UPI, cards and COD, clear delivery times, a visible WhatsApp contact and fast mobile pages, since most buyers arrive from Instagram or YouTube on a phone.

### Kongu Tamil, Tamil and English

Western Tamil Nadu buyers often respond to brands that sound like they come from the region. Kongu Tamil has its own words and warmth, and a short video where the owner speaks naturally from the factory floor can build more trust than a polished studio ad.

What to test first:

- The same product video with Kongu Tamil, standard Tamil and English voice-over
- The owner or a worker showing how the product is made in the unit
- Captions in Tanglish against plain English
- Offers timed to Pongal, Aadi, Deepavali and the wedding season

Keep the offer identical across versions and compare cost per delivered order by language. A fluent Tamil writer from the region should write or check every line; a literal translation from English sounds stiff.

## Step 7: Know your numbers before you scale

Factory owners know cost per unit well. Retail adds new lines that are easy to miss. Before you raise ad spend, list:

| Cost line | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Product | Unit cost, retail packaging, inserts |
| Fulfilment | Packing material, labour, storage |
| Shipping | Forward charges, COD fees, RTO returns |
| Platform | Store plan, apps, payment gateway or marketplace fees |
| Marketing | Ad spend, creators, samples, photography |
| After-sales | Replacements, service visits, customer support time |

## Step 8: Grow beyond Coimbatore

When repeat orders hold steady in western Tamil Nadu, widen the market one step at a time:

- **Chennai, Bengaluru and Kerala:** close by, with similar buying habits for many products.
- **Amazon and Flipkart:** buyers already searching for your category.
- **Quick commerce:** for fast-moving food and household items once supply is dependable.
- **The Tamil diaspora:** Singapore, Malaysia, the Gulf, the UK and North America can suit kitchen appliances, food and textiles.

## Mistakes Coimbatore makers often make

- Putting the whole B2B catalogue online instead of one or two heroes
- Using wholesale flat-lay photos as retail ads
- Pricing online below dealer prices without warning dealers
- Leaving online orders to whoever is free in the factory
- Running ads before COD checks, returns policy and tracking are ready

## How to start a D2C brand in Coimbatore: a checklist

- Hero product chosen and tested with real buyers
- Brand, pricing and accounts kept separate from B2B
- GST, trademark, product licences and Legal Metrology labels checked
- Packing space or 3PL set up, courier aggregator live
- COD confirmation on WhatsApp and RTO tracking in place
- Store live with UPI, cards, COD and a clear returns policy
- Kongu Tamil, Tamil and English creative tested
- Margin per delivered order known before scaling ads
