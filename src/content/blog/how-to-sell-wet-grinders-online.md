## Why wet grinders sell differently online

A wet grinder is a heavy, long-life kitchen appliance. Buyers often purchase one only once in many years, so they research carefully. They want to know:

- Will it grind enough batter for my family?
- Will the stones last, and can I get spares?
- What happens if the motor fails after a year?
- Is it easy to clean and to lift?

In a showroom a salesperson answers these questions. Online, your listing and videos must do it. That is the core of selling wet grinders online.

## Step 1: Understand the GI name before you use it

The Coimbatore Wet Grinder GI covers grinders produced in the defined area to the specification in the registration. Using the GI name or logo generally needs registration as an authorised user.

- Check whether your unit is registered as an authorised user, or can apply.
- Do not use the GI logo, or phrases that suggest GI status, unless you are allowed to.
- If you can use it, explain it simply on the product page: where it is made and what that means to the buyer.

The GI is context and trust, not a substitute for clear specs and service.

## Step 2: Check BIS and Legal Metrology basics

### BIS

Some household electrical appliances and components are covered by BIS quality control orders, and the list of covered products changes. Check with BIS or a compliance adviser whether your grinder, or its motor, needs a BIS licence or registration. Marketplaces may ask for documents, and listings can be removed if they are missing.

### Legal Metrology

Every retail pack needs the standard declarations: name and address of the manufacturer or marketer, the common name of the product, net quantity, month and year of manufacture, MRP and consumer care contact. Make sure the box, the listing and the invoice tell the same story.

### Electrical safety on the page

Explain safe use plainly: earthing, not running the grinder empty, how to clean without wetting the motor, and the voltage it is built for. This also reduces avoidable service calls.

## Step 3: Write listings led by specs

### Titles

Put the details buyers filter by into the title: brand, type (table-top, tilting or conventional), drum capacity, motor wattage and one standout feature. Avoid stuffing it with every keyword.

### The spec table

| Spec | Why buyers care |
|---|---|
| Drum capacity (litres) | Matches family size and batch size |
| Stone type and number | Affects grinding texture and life |
| Motor wattage and type | Suggests grinding speed and load |
| Voltage and plug | Essential for buyers abroad |
| Body and drum material | Durability and ease of cleaning |
| Dimensions and weight | Counter space and lifting |
| In the box | Atta kneader, coconut scraper, spare parts |
| Warranty | Motor, body and stones, stated separately |

### Images and video

Show the stones up close, the drum removed, the controls, the grinder on a real kitchen counter and a person lifting the drum. A short demo video of idli or dosa batter being ground, with the time it took, answers more questions than any paragraph. Record videos in Tamil and English, and keep claims to what the video shows.

### Honest comparisons

Buyers compare table-top and tilting models, two-litre and larger drums, and conventional stones against newer designs. A fair comparison chart of your own models, explaining who each suits, builds trust and reduces returns. Do not make claims about competitors you cannot back up.

## Step 4: Make warranty and service clear

Service is often the deciding factor for wet grinders. Write it in plain words on every listing and on your store:

1. What the warranty covers: motor, body, stones and electrical parts, with separate periods if they differ
2. What it does not cover: misuse, wrong voltage, physical damage
3. How to raise a claim: WhatsApp number, phone, email and hours
4. How service happens: technician visit, service centre or pickup
5. Where spares come from: stones, belts, gaskets and handles, and how to order them

If you rely on dealers for service in some areas, say so honestly and give the dealer list. If an area has no service, say what you offer instead, such as a pickup or a parts kit.

## Step 5: Pack for a heavy, fragile product

A wet grinder is heavy and the stones can crack if the box is dropped. Packing decides your damage rate and reviews.

- Fix the stones and drum so they cannot move inside the box.
- Use strong corrugated boxes with corner and edge protection.
- Mark boxes as heavy and fragile, with "this way up" arrows.
- Record a short packing video for each order to support courier claims.
- Track damage and returns by courier and pin code, and act on repeats.

## Step 6: Choose your channels

### Amazon and Flipkart

Most buyers start here. Use the right category, fill in every attribute, and build reviews from real buyers. Sponsored product ads on search terms such as "wet grinder 2 litre" and "table top wet grinder" can help new listings get seen; our [Amazon PPC guide for India](/blog/amazon-ppc-guide-india/) covers the basics. ### Your own store

Your store is where you explain the product fully, sell spares and accessories, and keep customer contacts for service reminders. Add a service request form, a spares page and a WhatsApp button. Repeat sales of stones, belts and accessories often come through the store.

### Dealers and B2B

Online sales do not replace dealers. Keep online prices in line with dealer prices, and consider routing local service through dealers. If you also sell commercial grinders to hotels and caterers, our [B2B digital marketing](/b2b-digital-marketing/) work covers lead generation for that side.

## Step 7: Reach diaspora kitchens abroad

Tamil and South Indian families in Singapore, Malaysia, the Gulf, the UK, the US, Canada and Australia still make idli and dosa batter at home, and many want a proper wet grinder.

Before you sell abroad, check:

- **Voltage and plug:** 230V suits many countries; North America needs models built for 110 to 120V.
- **Local electrical rules:** some countries require approvals for imported appliances.
- **Shipping weight:** heavy parcels are costly to send; price for it honestly.
- **Warranty abroad:** decide whether you replace parts by post, refund or use a local partner.

## Common mistakes

- Thin listings that leave out stone type, voltage or warranty details
- Using the GI name without checking authorised user status
- Weak packing that leads to cracked stones and one-star reviews
- No clear service route outside the home district
- Selling abroad without checking voltage and plugs

## How to sell wet grinders online: a checklist

- GI name used only if allowed
- BIS and Legal Metrology requirements checked with an adviser
- Spec table, photos and demo video on every listing
- Warranty and service written in plain words
- Packing tested for heavy, fragile shipping
- Amazon, Flipkart and own store set up, with spares on the store
- Voltage, plugs and warranty planned before any export
