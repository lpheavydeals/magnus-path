If you are working out how to start a pet food brand, UAE conditions shape almost every decision you will make. Most pet food on sale here is imported, summers are long and fierce, many owners live in apartments, and the people buying come from dozens of countries with brands they already trusted at home. That mix leaves room for a focused newcomer, provided the paperwork is in order, the label speaks to local owners in Arabic and English, and the second order is easier than going back to the old bag. This guide covers each stage, from choosing a business model to building repeat orders.

## Step 1: Decide what kind of pet food business you are building

Settle the model before you think about recipes, because it decides who holds the permits, who carries stock and where your margin sits.

- **Your own brand, made abroad and imported.** You develop recipes with a manufacturer overseas, ship finished packs and sell under your name. This is how most new UAE pet food brands begin.
- **Distribution of another maker's brand.** You handle the local paperwork, stock and sales, but the recipe and the brand equity belong to someone else. It is quicker to start and harder to build value in.
- **Local production.** Possible for some baked treats or fresh food, but it needs suitable premises and approvals from the relevant emirate. Few founders begin here.

Write down who will be the importer of record, who will register each product and where stock will be stored. A licensed importer or distributor with pet food experience can carry much of this load during your first year.

### Find a gap owners already notice

International labels fill the aisles at Carrefour and the pet store chains, so a head-on fight over complete dry dog food is expensive. Narrower openings are easier to own:

- Indoor cats in apartments, a very common household set-up in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
- Small and toy breeds whose owners want smaller kibble and smaller bags.
- Single-protein treats and toppers for fussy eaters.
- Lower-calorie training treats for dogs whose walks get shorter in summer, backed by the recipe rather than a promise.

Read the reviews on the leading Amazon.ae and Noon listings in your format. Complaints about broken kibble, smell after delivery, bags too big to store or food going stale in the heat are a free product brief. Groomers, trainers and rescue volunteers hear owners' frustrations every week and will usually tell you which products sit unsold.

## Step 2: Build the recipe with a manufacturer that ships to the Gulf

Choose a manufacturer that already exports pet food, ideally to the GCC. It will understand the documents importers ask for and how long approvals can take.

### Questions to ask before you sign

- Have you shipped pet food to the UAE or another GCC country, and which documents did the importer request?
- Can you provide health or origin certificates for animal-derived ingredients from the exporting country's authority?
- Could any ingredient, such as pork-derived material, cause problems at import? Ask early, not after printing.
- How was shelf life tested, and at what storage temperature?
- What are the minimum order quantities for each recipe and pack size, and what are the lead times?
- Who owns the recipe if production moves later?

A complete food, meant to be fed on its own, should be formulated by a qualified animal nutritionist against recognised nutritional guidelines. Treats and toppers are complementary foods and simpler to launch, but they still need a sensible feeding guide.

### Plan for heat from the first sample

Containers wait at ports, delivery vans cook in July and parcels can sit outside a villa gate for hours. Fats in food can turn rancid faster when stored hot, so ask for shelf-life data that reflects warm storage, choose barrier packaging, and agree that your warehouse keeps pet food in temperature-controlled space. Order a sample shipment and open packs after a few weeks in realistic conditions before you commit to volume.

## Step 3: Permits, registration and Arabic labels

This is where most launch delays come from. Treat what follows as general orientation, not legal advice: requirements change, differ by product type and can vary between emirates, so confirm every point with your importer, a customs broker or a regulatory adviser.

### Federal import permits and registration

Pet food and animal feed brought into the UAE generally falls under the Ministry of Climate Change and Environment. Importers typically need an import permit for consignments, and products often have to be registered before the first shipment, supported by manufacturer documents, ingredient lists and health certificates for animal-origin ingredients. Consignments may be inspected or sampled at the port of entry.

### Municipal and emirate food rules

On top of federal rules, the food safety authority or municipality in the emirate where you store and sell may run its own controls. These can include product or label registration, warehouse inspections and checks on how stock is handled. Ask your importer which authorities apply to your warehouse location and sales channels.

### What the label should carry

Arabic text is generally expected, printed on the pack or added through a compliant sticker, alongside English. Design both languages together so a sticker never hides the nutrient analysis panel or feeding table.

| Label element | Why it matters to UAE owners |
|---|---|
| Product name, complete or complementary | Sets feeding expectations from the start |
| Species and life stage | The first filter owners apply on Noon and Amazon.ae |
| Ingredients or composition | Owners compare these line by line |
| Nutrient analysis panel | Protein, fat, fibre, ash and moisture in a familiar format |
| Feeding guide in kilograms and grams | Imported packs often show pounds or cups |
| Net weight, batch, production and expiry dates | Needed for checks and for trust |
| Manufacturer and UAE importer details | Shows who stands behind the product |
| Storage advice | "Store in a cool, dry place" matters here |

### Keep claims on the food side of the line

Describe ingredients, nutrient content and feeding. Avoid verbs such as "cures", "prevents" or "heals", never say a food treats a condition, and do not imply any endorsement you cannot document. Wording that sounds medicinal can move a product towards veterinary rules with separate registration. Point health questions to the owner's vet in every channel.

### Business basics

You will need a trade licence that covers trading in pet food, from a free zone or the mainland depending on how you plan to sell. Register for VAT at 5% once you pass the threshold, protect your brand with a UAE trade mark, and arrange product liability cover. We are not lawyers or accountants, so take local professional advice.

## Step 4: Design packs for apartments, couriers and the summer

**Front of pack.** Species, life stage, main protein and weight should be readable on a small thumbnail, because that is how most owners first see it on Noon and Amazon.ae.

**Both languages.** Arabic reads right to left, so plan the layout, not just the translation. Have the Arabic copy written or checked by a fluent Arabic writer.

**Pack sizes.** Apartment storage is limited, and heavy sacks are awkward to carry from the car park. Offer a sensible small size for trial and a larger size for regulars.

**Protection.** Resealable closures and barrier film keep treats fresh once opened. Send sample packs through local couriers in July and see what arrives.

## Step 5: Choose where to sell

### Your own store

A Shopify store in Arabic and English, with right-to-left layouts, gives you subscriptions, bundles and direct contact with owners. Offer cash on delivery but confirm orders on WhatsApp, and consider Tabby or Tamara for larger bundles. Delivery slots matter for heavy bags. ### Amazon.ae and Noon

Many owners compare food on Amazon.ae and Noon before buying anywhere else. Build listings with label images in both languages, every attribute filled and variations by size and flavour. Expect the platforms to ask for import and registration documents for food categories.

### Talabat and Careem quick commerce

Quick commerce catches the owner who notices the bag is empty at night. You usually reach it through a distributor that holds stock in the right areas, so start with your fastest-moving packs.

### Stockists

Independent pet shops, groomers and clinics with retail shelves are realistic first stockists and an honest source of feedback. Carrefour and the large chains set price expectations, but they rarely suit a brand in its first year.

## Step 6: Plan around the UAE calendar

- **Ramadan.** Household routines move later into the evening; adjust delivery slots and send reminders at times owners are awake and ordering.
- **Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha.** Families travel, so stock-up offers before the holiday and boarding-friendly packs work well.
- **Summer.** Demand drops as residents travel. Promote pausing rather than cancelling, and publish practical content on keeping pets cool indoors, with medical questions sent to the vet.
- **Back to school in late August.** Routines return and reorders recover; plan a welcome-back message.
- **White Friday and Noon's Yellow Friday.** Good for larger bags and multi-packs.
- **UAE National Day and the Dubai Shopping Festival.** Cooler weather brings outdoor activity and gift sets.

## Step 7: Make the second order the easy option

- **Trial packs** lower the risk for owners who fear their pet will refuse something new.
- **A switching guide** shows owners how to blend the new food into the old one gradually across about a week.
- **A day-seven check-in** on WhatsApp or email, sent with consent, catches problems early.
- **Reminders timed to the pack** arrive a few days before it is likely to run out.
- **Subscriptions owners control**, with skip and pause buttons that work during summer travel.

## How to start a pet food brand: UAE launch timeline

**Months 1 and 2.** Fix the model and niche, appoint an importer, shortlist manufacturers and run sample batches.

**Months 3 and 4.** Submit permits and registrations, finalise bilingual labels, order packaging, build the store and listings, and photograph real pets in UAE homes.

**Months 5 and 6.** Launch with modest budgets on Meta, Snapchat, Amazon.ae and Noon, collect reviews, switch on reminders, then add quick commerce once stock is steady. Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC can follow later, after checking each country's own rules.

### Mistakes to avoid

- Printing thousands of packs before the Arabic label is reviewed.
- Shipping stock before registration is confirmed.
- Copying health wording from overseas packaging.
- Storing food in a warehouse without temperature control.
- Making subscriptions hard to pause during summer.
