If you want to know how to sell perfume online, start with the problem every fragrance brand faces: the buyer cannot smell the product. In a shop they spray a tester, wait a few minutes and decide. Online they see a bottle and a list of notes. Everything that works in online perfume selling, from product pages to sampling to ads, is a way of closing that gap. This guide covers the product range, licensing, how to describe a scent, samples and discovery sets, shipping alcohol-based perfume, attar, the trademark risks of "inspired by" naming and how to grow.

We are an ecommerce growth team, not lawyers. Check the regulatory and trademark points below with a qualified adviser.

## Decide what you are selling

Fragrance is a broad category, and the choices you make early shape everything else.

| Product type | What it is | Online selling notes |
|---|---|---|
| Eau de parfum (EDP) | Higher concentration of perfume oil in alcohol | Longer wear; usually the hero format |
| Eau de toilette (EDT) | Lighter concentration | Fresh, daily-wear scents and lower price points |
| Body mist | Light, often sweet, low concentration | Impulse buys and gifting |
| Attar | Concentrated perfume oil, usually alcohol-free | Strong tradition; easier to ship than alcohol-based perfume |
| Solid perfume and roll-ons | Wax or oil base | Travel-friendly; good add-on products |
| Discovery sets and minis | Small vials of several scents | The entry point for new buyers |

A small, clear range sells better than a long one. Most new brands launch with four to eight scents across a few families (fresh, floral, woody, oud, musk, gourmand) and a discovery set that contains all of them.

## Licensing and labels

In India, perfumes are generally regulated as cosmetics. The maker needs a cosmetics manufacturing licence from the state licensing authority, and imported perfumes need import registration with the central regulator. Many D2C brands work with a contract manufacturer that holds the licence.

Labels typically need the product name, manufacturer details, batch number, manufacturing or expiry date where relevant, ingredients and warnings, plus legal metrology details such as MRP, net quantity and a customer care contact. Alcohol-based perfume adds two points to check with your adviser: some states regulate the use of alcohol in manufacturing, and flammability warnings belong on the pack.

Keep the label as your source of truth. Listings and product pages should match it, including the volume and concentration.

## How to sell perfume online with notes, mood and occasion

Most buyers do not know fragrance vocabulary, but they understand moods and moments. A strong product page does three things.

### Name the notes, then translate them

List the top, heart and base notes, then explain them in everyday words: bright and citrusy for the first few minutes, softly floral through the day, warm and woody by evening. Keep the pyramid short; five or six notes are easier to imagine than fifteen.

### Be honest about strength and wear time

State the concentration and give a realistic idea of how long the scent lasts and how far it projects. Say that wear time varies with skin, weather and clothing. Overstating longevity is one of the quickest ways to collect poor reviews.

### Suggest occasions and comparisons

Tell buyers when to wear the scent: office, evening, weddings, summer days. Offer comparisons that guide without naming another brand, such as "if you like warm, spicy scents, start here". A short quiz that asks about preferred moods and occasions can recommend two or three scents and send buyers to the discovery set.

### Show people, not only bottles

Short videos of real people describing a scent in their own words often do more than a polished bottle shot. Reviews that describe how a scent wore through the day are worth asking for specifically.

## Discovery sets and samples

Sampling is the most dependable way to lower the risk of a first order.

- **Discovery set.** Several scents in small vials, priced so buyers see it as a fair trial.
- **Credit back.** Offer the set's cost as credit on a later full-bottle order, so the sample feels like a first step rather than an extra cost.
- **Free sample with every order.** Add a vial of a different scent to each parcel, chosen to match what the buyer bought.
- **Follow-up.** A WhatsApp or email message a few days after delivery asks which scent they liked and links straight to that full bottle.

Track how many sample buyers go on to buy a full bottle and how long it takes. That number tells you how much you can afford to spend acquiring a sample buyer.

## Shipping perfume safely

Shipping is where many perfume brands get caught out.

### Dangerous goods

Alcohol-based perfume is usually classed as a flammable liquid for transport. Couriers and aggregators often accept it only by surface transport, or with dangerous-goods declarations, specific packaging and quantity limits. Some do not carry it by air at all. Ask every courier you use for its current rules in writing, label parcels correctly and keep the safety data sheets your manufacturer provides. Marketplaces also run their own dangerous-goods reviews for perfume listings and may ask for these documents.

Oil-based attars are often easier to ship, but do not assume; check their classification too.

### Packaging

- Use bottles with secure caps and add a seal or shrink band.
- Pack bottles upright in fitted inserts so they cannot move.
- Put each bottle in a sealed bag in case it leaks.
- Test parcels by sending them to your own team in different cities before launch.

### Delivery times and costs

Surface transport is slower, so set delivery expectations clearly at checkout and in confirmation messages. Heavier glass bottles also raise shipping costs; our [free shipping threshold calculator](/tools/free-shipping-threshold-calculator/) helps you set a threshold that protects margin.

### Returns

Opened perfume usually cannot be resold. Most brands accept returns only for damaged, leaking or wrong items, and some allow exchange of unopened bottles. Sampling reduces disappointment-driven returns more than any policy.

## Selling attar online

Attar buyers are buying a tradition. They want to know the base oil, the main ingredient (rose, oud, sandalwood, vetiver, mitti) and how the attar was made, along with the place it comes from, such as Kannauj. Explain how to apply it, how little is needed and how it wears compared with alcohol-based perfume.

Attar works well in roll-on bottles and small sizes, which suit sampling and gifting. Ramadan and Eid are strong periods for attar, alongside Diwali and the wedding season. Avoid health or spiritual claims in ads; describe the scent, the craft and the occasion.

## "Inspired by" perfumes and trademark risk

Many brands make scents that smell similar to well-known designer fragrances. The scent itself is not the main risk. The name is.

- Using a designer brand's name in product titles, ad text, keywords, hashtags or URLs can lead to trademark complaints.
- Marketplaces act quickly on complaints and may remove listings or suspend accounts.
- Ad platforms may reject ads or restrict bidding on trademarked terms.
- Copying bottle shapes, logos or packaging adds further risk.

The safer approach is to give each scent its own name and describe it by notes, mood and occasion. Build your own brand rather than borrowing someone else's. Take advice from a trademark lawyer before launching a range like this, and register your own brand name early.

## Choosing sales channels

| Channel | Good for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Your own store | Discovery sets, credits, gifting, customer data | You drive all the traffic yourself |
| Amazon | Search-led demand and gifting | Dangerous-goods review, counterfeit sellers |
| Nykaa and Myntra | Beauty and fashion shoppers | Content standards, margins |
| Instagram and WhatsApp | Discovery, creator content, repeat orders | Needs a clear path to checkout |

## Growing through the year

Perfume is a popular gift, so sales peak around Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Valentine's Day, Eid and the wedding season. Prepare gift sets, wrapping options and delivery cut-off dates well ahead, because surface shipping adds days. Between peaks, keep self-buyers engaged with new launches, layering ideas and refill reminders.

## Pricing and unit economics

Perfume can carry healthy margins, but the costs are easy to underestimate. Before you set prices, add up:

- Perfume oil, alcohol or carrier oil, and blending
- Bottle, atomiser, cap, box and inserts, which often cost more than the juice
- Samples and discovery-set vials given away or sold below cost
- Dangerous-goods shipping, which may cost more than standard parcels
- Breakage, leaks and replacements
- Marketplace fees and returns
- Advertising to reach a buyer who has never smelt your scents

Then look at the full customer, not only the first order. A buyer who starts with a discovery set, moves to a full bottle and reorders twice a year can justify a higher acquisition cost than a one-off gift buyer. Track both groups separately.

## How to sell perfume online: a launch checklist

- Range of four to eight scents across clear families, plus a discovery set
- Licence route confirmed and labels checked against legal metrology rules
- Every scent named by you, with no designer names in titles, ads or tags
- Product pages with notes in everyday words, concentration, honest wear time and occasions
- Sample credit and follow-up messages set up before ads start
- Courier rules for dangerous goods confirmed in writing, and packaging tested
- Return policy for opened, damaged and leaking items written plainly
- Gift sets and delivery cut-offs planned for the next gifting peak
