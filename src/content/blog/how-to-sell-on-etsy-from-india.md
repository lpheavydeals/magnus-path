If you are wondering how to sell on Etsy from India, the short answer is: open a seller account with your Indian bank details, list products that suit overseas buyers, sort out export shipping, and write listings the way foreign shoppers search. Etsy is a global marketplace for handmade, vintage and craft-supply items, which makes it a natural fit for Indian artisans and small makers. This guide walks through each step, from account setup to pricing, shipping, Etsy SEO and the point where your own store makes sense.

## Why Etsy suits Indian sellers

Etsy buyers come looking for things they cannot find in a regular shop. They value craft, origin and story, and many are happy to pay for it. That plays to India's strengths: a deep tradition of handloom, metalwork, painting and jewellery, and makers who can produce small batches by hand.

Etsy also handles the hard parts of reaching buyers abroad. Traffic, checkout, buyer payments and basic trust signals like reviews come built in. You focus on product, photos, listings and dispatch.

The trade-off is that Etsy is a crowded marketplace with fees on every sale, and you do not own the customer relationship. Treat it as a strong first channel, not your only one. For most small makers, learning how to sell on Etsy from India is also a low-risk way to test which products overseas buyers actually want before investing in a website or trade fairs.

## Setting up your Etsy seller account in India

Account setup is straightforward. Keep these ready before you start:

- **Email and a shop name.** Pick a name you can also use as a brand and, later, a website domain.
- **Identity details.** Etsy may ask for identity verification, such as your PAN or other ID, depending on its current rules.
- **Indian bank account.** Payouts from Etsy are sent to the bank account you add.
- **A card for fees.** Etsy may ask for a card for billing, depending on your account.
- **Shop policies.** Returns, exchanges, processing time and custom-order terms.
- **Your first few listings.** Etsy asks you to create at least one listing to open the shop. Aim to launch with several, so the shop does not look empty.

Fill in the "About" section properly. Overseas buyers want to know who makes the product, where, and how. A short story with a photo of your workshop or artisans builds trust quickly.

## What sells on Etsy from India

The best products for an Etsy seller in India share three traits: they are handmade or have a clear cultural origin, they are light and safe to ship, and they are hard to find in shops abroad. Categories that fit well include:

| Category | Examples | Shipping notes |
|---|---|---|
| Handloom and textiles | Cotton and silk stoles, block-printed fabric, cushion covers, table linen | Light and easy to pack |
| Traditional art | Tanjore paintings, Madhubani, Pattachitra, Kalamkari | Needs rigid packing; framed pieces are heavy |
| Brass and metal decor | Brass lamps, idols, bells, bowls | Heavier, so price shipping carefully |
| Jewellery | Silver, oxidised, beaded and temple-style jewellery | Small and valuable; use tracked shipping |
| Craft supplies | Beads, sari ribbon, yarn, wooden printing blocks | Popular with makers abroad |
| Leather and paper goods | Handmade journals, wallets, bags | Light and durable |

Before you commit stock, research demand. Search Etsy the way a buyer in the US or UK would, for example "brass diya" or "handmade block print tablecloth". Note which listings show many reviews, what price range they sit in, and how their photos look. Avoid categories with strict rules abroad, such as food, cosmetics or anything with restricted materials, until you understand the destination country's requirements.

## Getting paid in India

Etsy collects payment from the buyer and pays out to your Indian bank account after deducting its fees. Buyers can pay in their own currency, and Etsy converts the amount for your payout. Payout timing, conversion and any reserve rules follow Etsy's current policy, so read the payments section of your shop settings rather than relying on old blog posts.

Keep clean records from day one. Download monthly sales and fee reports, match payouts to orders, and share them with your accountant. Export income has its own GST treatment, and many exporters file a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) to export without paying IGST upfront. Ask a chartered accountant what applies to your business.

## Shipping and customs basics

Shipping is where most new Indian Etsy sellers struggle. Plan it before your first sale.

### Choose your shipping route

Your options usually include India Post's international services, international courier companies and courier aggregators that compare rates across carriers. India Post can be economical for light parcels, while couriers are faster and offer better tracking. Many sellers use both depending on order value and destination.

### Paperwork and IEC

For commercial export shipments, you will generally need an Importer Exporter Code (IEC) from DGFT. It is applied for online and linked to your PAN. You will also need an invoice for each parcel with a clear product description, value and HS code. Requirements differ by route and change from time to time, so confirm them with your courier or post office.

### Duties in the buyer's country

The buyer's country may charge import duty or tax, and rules in some markets, including the US, have changed recently. Decide whether duties are paid by the buyer on delivery or included in your price, and say so clearly in your listing and shop policy. Unexpected charges at the door are a common cause of refused parcels and bad reviews.

### Packing

Pack for a long journey with multiple handlings. Use rigid boxes for art and metalware, moisture protection for textiles, and secure packing for jewellery. Good packaging also acts as part of the brand experience.

## Pricing for international buyers

Many Indian sellers underprice on Etsy because they think in rupees and Indian retail prices. International buyers compare you with other Etsy sellers, not with an Indian bazaar. Build your price from your real costs:

1. **Product cost:** materials, labour and your margin.
2. **Packaging:** boxes, protection, branded inserts.
3. **Shipping:** the actual rate to each main destination.
4. **Etsy fees:** listing, transaction and payment processing fees. Check Etsy's current fee page, because rates change.
5. **Currency conversion:** a small allowance for conversion costs and exchange-rate movement.
6. **Returns and damage:** an allowance for the occasional lost or damaged parcel.
7. **Advertising:** if you use Etsy Ads or are enrolled in offsite ads, budget for it.

Decide whether to offer free shipping with the cost built into the product price, or to charge shipping separately. Many buyers prefer seeing free shipping, but the money still has to come from somewhere. Run the numbers per product, not across the whole shop.

## Etsy SEO: titles, tags and photos

Etsy search decides who sees your listings. Etsy SEO works on the same principle as Google: match how buyers search, then convince them to click and buy.

### Titles

Put the main search phrase near the start, in plain English that overseas buyers use. "Handmade brass diya oil lamp, traditional Indian decor" is better than "Beautiful Diwali special item". Avoid long lists of repeated keywords.

### Tags

Etsy lets you add a set number of tags per listing (13 at the time of writing; check the listing editor). Use all of them. Mix product terms ("block print tablecloth"), materials ("cotton"), styles ("boho"), occasions ("housewarming gift") and recipients ("gift for her"). Use multi-word phrases rather than single words.

### Attributes and categories

Choose the most specific category and fill every attribute: colour, material, occasion, style. Etsy uses these to match listings with filtered searches.

### Photos and video

Photos sell on Etsy more than words do. Use every photo slot: a clean main image on a plain background, close-ups of texture and craft, a scale shot in a hand or room, and a lifestyle image. Add a short video if you can. Shoot in natural light and keep colours true, so "not as described" complaints stay low.

### Description and reviews

Answer buyer questions in the description: size, material, care, processing time and who pays duties. Reviews strongly affect visibility, so ship on time, communicate clearly, and follow up politely after delivery.

## Common mistakes Indian Etsy sellers make

Most problems for new sellers come from a short list of avoidable mistakes:

- **Launching with too few listings.** A shop with three items looks untested. More listings also mean more chances to appear in search.
- **Underpricing.** Low prices leave nothing for fees, shipping or the occasional lost parcel, and can make buyers doubt quality.
- **Vague processing times.** If an item is made to order, say so and give a realistic dispatch date.
- **Ignoring messages.** Overseas buyers often ask about size, colour or customisation before buying. Reply quickly, allowing for time-zone differences.
- **Photos that hide the craft.** Dark or cluttered photos undersell handmade detail.

Fix these and the basics of how to sell on Etsy from India become much easier to manage.

## When to move to your own Shopify store

Etsy is a strong starting point, but you rent the audience. Fees apply to every sale, the platform controls search visibility, and you have limited ways to market to past buyers.

Signs that you are ready for your own store:

- You have steady monthly sales and repeat buyers.
- Buyers search for your shop name directly.
- You want to sell wholesale, run your own ads or build an email list.
- You plan to sell through Instagram, Google or other marketplaces too.
